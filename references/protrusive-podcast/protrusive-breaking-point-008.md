[17:39] And I'm just going to say that one of the classical features of certain aspects of neurodivergence is perfectionism. And perfectionism is one of the major traits of suicidal thinking. And there are a number of traits that move towards suicidal thinking, but there are two that are particularly pertinent to dentistry. And one is the perfectionist trait. And but there's another one which figures frequently in those who think about suicide. And that's breaking our moral code. And we can cope with breaking your moral code um if it happens infrequently. But if you're working in an environment where you want to work at this level, but the workplace is causing you to work at this level, then you cannot be in that environment without it having significant impact on your mental well-being.
