[16:08] So, all of that's in there, but we forget one other thing here and that is that dentists and DCPs are just ordinary people as well. They turn up on a Monday morning and they get on with their professional stuff and all this stuff that's hitting them um that was just talked about, but they're also ordinary people. And they've got marriage difficulties and they've got cancer diagnoses and they've got kids that are struggling at school and they've got all of that stuff going on and a failure in a mortgage payment, all of that stuff's happening. And then here we are, the erudite professional, we step in through the practice or through the clinic on a Monday morning and the smile goes on and we have to carry all of that stuff because we couldn't possibly let it out, could we? Could we? Because, my goodness, what then happens if we let it all out? Well, here's the challenge. What does happen if you let it all out? What happens if you take your partner in the practice aside and say, "John, Jane, Mary, Tom, I'm really struggling just now and I'm going to have to take 2 to 3 weeks off until I get um things sorted out." You can't do that.
