[34:03] [Shabnam] I'm a bit of a control freak. I can't delegate stuff but you need good people to delegate to. What I would say is if you don't like compliance it's okay. Now there's so many things out there that you can delegated. It's a task. It can be delegated. You can hire someone to do it, but at the end of the day, it doesn't matter whether you set it up yourself in calendar tasks or whether you have a compliance portal to do, there is a human being that has to do it. If you don't want to do it, you just have to hire someone to do it. And you've got to hire someone that's meticulous and organized and and persistent cuz getting people to sign policies every year is soul destroying.
