[15:29] [Shabnam] we knew in the future they were going to be bought out by corporates and that practice that I was going to buy is now so expensive I couldn't afford it anyway so I knew I was a brilliant associate I built the practice up but at the end of the day none of that goodwill belonged to me and then I worked in Wimpol Street so I was working NHS one day working on Wimpol Street the next day it was like seeing an exempt patient and then charging £400 for a checkup. Like when I say I've done it all, like I just went out there and tried stuff. And what it made me realize is that for me, the patient's the most important thing. And for me to be able to control their environment, how they're treated, kind of really just building what I wanted, how my patients be treated, and have that job security for myself. That was the reason I set up a practice so I could practice dentistry how I like to do it without anyone telling me I can't.
