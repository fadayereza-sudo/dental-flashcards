[28:30] Now, and here's a really important further point to this, and it's this is the pivotal one. So, every healthcare professional that I have ever asked that question to has said, "Oh, John, don't be daft. Don't be silly. Yes, things are bad at the moment, but let's go and have a glass of chilled Merlot or a beer. It'll be fine in the morning." Okay? Never take that Never take that response from a fellow healthcare professional. And in the Canwell Trust, we call it the double bounce. So, you bounce the ball once, and you ask the question, are you thinking about taking your life? And you get the response back, "Don't be daft. Don't be silly." And you bounce the ball again, and you ask it again, "No, this is really important to me. I can see that you're struggling. Are you thinking about taking your life?" And the response of, "Yes, I am. I need help." on that second bounce is profoundly greater than the first bounce.
