[38:09] [Shabnam] firing people for me was very difficult. I do the firing in the practice and the reason I do it is because I wanted to challenge myself. So I had to find a way to do it that I felt comfortable with. And the way I did it is with metrics. So I measure things. I have my values. And often because I measure things so well, the people that are on probation normally come to me and say, "I'm not doing very well. I think I should leave." Which is great, right? So I found a system now where people themselves identify that they don't fit with my practice. Not that they I never hire anyone that's bad. I have lovely staff. But they identify themselves. They don't have the right values to fit with how the rest of the team works.
