[7:43] Seb Tracey: So, use of home as office is another really common expense that people tend to forget about or don't really think about too much. So, we do add it in automatically but it's something that clients don't really come to us expecting. And whilst we claim to standard amounts particularly when you get to that practice owner level, there may be more complex calculations that we could do to basically increase the amount we can claim for the use of home as office. People forget how much they do work from home. I think people sort of come home from from surgery and think that's it for the day. But actually before they know it, they're sitting on their phones there doing emails, thinking about business, having a conversation, logging on probably to a laptop or PC or something and doing some planning, thinking about advertising.
