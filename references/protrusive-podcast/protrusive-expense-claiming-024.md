[52:27] Seb Tracey: If you bought a practice and you've taken out large bank finance, a lot of your income on paper is going to repay the capital of that large loan that you took out at the start. So, from a cash flow perspective, sometimes and what I sort of try and gear people buying their first practice up for is you might be more cash rich as an associate for a period of time, but as a principal, you're building a savings account. The practice is a bit of a savings account that when you come to sell that, you're then going to realize I mean, you've paid off the loan and are realizing a large sum on a large asset and and that's going to be a large injection into your life. But it is down the line. So, principals on paper do earn more. Cash wise, they might not be as well off as associates probably think.
