And in this case, I can see how for a young dentists just going from DFT, it can be easy to get caught up in the promise of the private treatment that lies ahead and just sort of meeting the patient's demands, doing the textbook thing of just putting a crown on it, but not actually appreciating that the tooth's been exposed for some time. And so in that case I ended up kind of doing a more minimally invasive thing and having kind of again a senior colleague there like an endodontist to sort of guide me really helps in these cases. But we ended up putting sort of like a permanent filling on the tooth, reviewing it, taking a review PA and sure enough there was kind of signs of asymptomatic apical periodontitis which was a bit more clear and he was the patient then at that point was you know happy to go ahead with a private RCT with our endodontist and he was actually quite thankful that he didn't jump into an expensive crown
