[38:40] [Shabnam] I think it's just I have high standards. Like I've had nurses in the past that don't want to perform to my standards and I don't want to come down to them. So I just say this is what I'm expecting and then I go this is what I would like you to do. I probably think the hardest thing for me is making time to have those conversations. You need to have them regularly. You need to document them. That's the hardest thing I find. If you document things well, then you know from a HR perspective, everything is easy because everything's been documented. You've shown you've given them opportunities and things like that.
