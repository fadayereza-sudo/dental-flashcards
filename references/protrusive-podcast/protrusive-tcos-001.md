[5:24] [Jaz] Do you think it's grown in the last so many years? [Emma] No, I think the complete opposite dental practices are really trying to get this TCO role into their businesses. I think the problem is with dentists now is they're so busy. The admin time it takes for a dentist to look after one patient is just, it's too much for one person to deal with and they have to have more discipline as a team to be able to do that for you. I mean the whole idea of a TCO is it's that middle person, so you've got your reception team, you've got your dentist, and you've got the middle person who's got the clinical knowledge, who can take some of the heat off the dentist but can also support the front of house team as well.
