When replacement dentures are to be provided draw up a 'shopping list' of aspects of the old dentures which are to be altered and aspects which are to be retained. This information is to be included in the Restorative Dentistry diagnostic sheet.

Normally, a design must be produced before other dental treatment is carried out because the type of denture is likely to influence the overall treatment plan, e.g. a rest seat may be incorporated into a Class II restoration; a full veneer crown may be contoured to provide an ideal shape for retention and support; a tooth may be extracted instead of restored because it has over-erupted and would interfere with the fitting of a partial denture.

Discuss with the patient any limitations of dentures which may have become apparent during the assessment stage.
