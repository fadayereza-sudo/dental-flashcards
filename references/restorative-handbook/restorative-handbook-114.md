## Bacterial infections

As a general rule, do not prescribe antibiotics unless there are signs of spreading infection (e.g. facial/neck swelling, trismus), systemic infection (e.g. fever, aches, chills, weakness), and discuss with tutor the need for it in immunocompromised patients (e.g. pt undergoing chemotherapy treatment, who received organ transplant, HIV+).

All doses stated are for adults. Below you will find the drug suggested on BNF and an alternative in case of allergy to the first drug, for more alternatives refer to BNF or 'Drug prescribing for Dentistry' (SDCEP) document. In some cases one might prescribe both Amoxicillin and Metronidazole.

### Periapical or Periodontal abscess

**Amoxicillin capsules, 500mg**
- 500mg 3 times daily for 5 days
- 15 capsules

**or Metronidazole tablets, 200mg**
- 200mg 3 times daily for 5 days
- 15 tablets
- Avoid alcohol as drug interaction will make you feel sick
- Do not prescribe for patients taking warfarin as interaction increases bleeding risk
- For severe infection, the dose of metronidazole should be doubled

### Pericoronitis or Acute necrotising ulcerative gingivitis

**Metronidazole tablets, 200mg**
- 200mg 3 times daily for 3 days
- 9 tablets

**or Amoxicillin capsules, 500mg**
- 500mg 3 times daily for 3 days
- 9 capsules
- Avoid alcohol as drug interaction will make you feel sick
- Do not prescribe for patients taking warfarin as interaction increases bleeding risk
- For severe infection, the dose of amoxicillin should be doubled
