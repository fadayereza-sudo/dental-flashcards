## Prilocaine hydrochloride 3% and felypressin 0.03 IU/ml - Citanest with Octapressin (Dentsply)

1 cartridge (2.2ml) contains: 66 mg of prilocaine and 1.188 μg (0.066 IU) of felypressin.
1ml contains: 30 mg of prilocaine and 0.54 μg (0.03 IU) of felypressin.

- **Indication:** When adrenaline must be avoided (i.e. patients with severe ischemic heart disease or recent myocardial infarction).
- **Dose:** Adults: Suggested maximum dose: 5 cartridges of 2.2ml in a healthy 70Kg adult. Do not exceed 9 mg/kg. Note: Felypressin can cause coronary vasoconstriction when used at high doses; limit dose in patients with coronary artery disease.
- **Length of anaesthesia:** Shorter duration when compared to articaine with adrenaline, similar to lidocaine with adrenaline and longer than mepivacaine plain, around 1.5 to 2.5 hours.
- **Pregnancy and lactation:** In large doses prilocaine may cause maternal and foetal methemoglobinaemia which could lead to foetal hypoxia. Present in milk but not known to be harmful.
- **Warnings and precautions:** Felypressin at high doses can cause coronary vasoconstriction. Limit dose in patients with coronary artery disease. May interact with other medicines: Anti arrhythmic medicines (i.e. mexiletine); Methaemoglobinaemia medicines (i.e. sulfonamides, antimalarials and certain nitric compounds); Overdose of prilocaine can cause methaemoglobinaemia.
- **Contraindications:** Allergy to components of this solution. Congenital or idiopathic methaemoglobinaemia.
