## Interocclusal records

The key to a good interocclusal record is that the technician can locate the upper and lower arches in exactly the same way they meet in the patients mouth, and that the models when put together are in one clear and stable relationship.

### ICP or RCP?

Whenever we are NOT trying to change the way the patient bites we should use an ICP record, only if changing the OVD or for patients without a stable reproducible record should an RCP record be used.

| Case | Record required |
|---|---|
| If models fit together in only one way that matches the patient | None |
| If models would have fitted together but you have prepped a tooth making it now not clear how they fit | Blu-mousse over top of prep and get patient to close together in ICP |
| If significant wear and no clear position for models | Full arch wax / Blu-mousse in RCP |
| If too many missing teeth and no stable position | Wax rims in edentulous spaces + Blu-mousse |
