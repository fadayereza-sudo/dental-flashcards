## Chronic atrophic candidiasis (denture stomatitis)

**Miconazole oral gel, 24 mg/ml**
- Apply 2.5ml (pea-size) 4 times daily, after meals for 5 to 7 days
- One 80g tube
- Apply to fitting surface of upper denture
- Continue treatment for at least 7 days after lesion or symptoms have disappeared
- Dentures should be taken out at night and brushed with gel
- Clean your denture(s) thoroughly and leave them out as often as possible during the treatment period
- New dentures may be required if these measures fail despite good compliance

**or Nystatin oral suspension, 100,000 units/ml** (if miconazole is contraindicated)
- 1ml 4 times daily for 7 days after meals
- One 30ml bottle and pipette
- Remove dentures and rinse mouth with 1ml After meals, hold close to lesion then swallow
- Continue treatment for 48h after lesion or symptoms have disappeared

Miconazole: do not prescribe for patients taking warfarin as interaction increases bleeding risk. Avoid (GP should be consulted) for patients taking statins as it may increase the risk of side effects such as liver and kidney damage. Avoid in pregnancy and breast feeding.
