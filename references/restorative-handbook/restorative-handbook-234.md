When required e.g. for 3 or more crowns, custom trays can be ordered from the laboratory. Custom trays are normally constructed in light cure acrylic with 6mm spacing, occlusal stops on selected non prepared teeth and a short handle. A range of disposable stock are available from the trolleys on the clinic. For single crowns custom trays are not required.

**Impression materials**

A single stage heavy and light bodied addition cured silicone technique is preferred.
