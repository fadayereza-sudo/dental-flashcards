## Past dental history (PDH)

A record of the patient's normal attendance pattern, oral hygiene regime, previous experiences at the dentist and if a denture wearer a full denture wearing history.

- Regular attender? When was the last visit?
- OH regime:
  - Brush how many times per day?
  - Toothbrush: Manual / Electric?
  - Mouthwash and toothpaste containing fluoride?
  - Floss / Interdental toothbrush?
- Are you anxious about dentistry? If yes, any previous bad experience(s)?
- Dentures:
  - Age of current denture(s)?
  - How many sets of dentures have you had over how many years? Which set was the most successful?
  - Do they cause discomfort?
  - Do you wear them at night?
  - Do you like the appearance of your present dentures? Can you eat with your present dentures?

## Social history (SH)

- Age and occupation
- Brief overview of current dietary habits
- Smoking habits (How many years smoking? How many per day? Previous attempt(s) to stop?)
- Use of smokeless tobacco?
- Drinking habits (How many units per week?)
- Availability for treatment
