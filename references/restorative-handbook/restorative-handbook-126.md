## Facebow recording

We use the Denar slidematic facebow system. Staff trained with a different system are invited to attend one of our regular 'Facebows and Articulators' CPD events.

### Material required

From dispensary: facebow, transfer jig, hot water & bowl, wax knife. From white trolley: Moyco extra-hard Beauty wax.

### Step-by-step guide

1. Using a ruler, make a mark on the right side of the patient's nose 42mm above the right lateral incisor edge.
2. Soften beauty wax and apply to bite fork creating a double layer on the top.
3. Re-soften the wax and place in patient's mouth, taking care that:
   - Midline notch is in the middle
   - Prong is pointing forwards below patient's right eye
   - Press quite but not too firmly, you are aiming just to create imprints of the cusp tips creating at least a tripod of indents that the upper model will fit into
4. Remove and allow to cool.
5. Place the bitefork back in the patient's mouth making sure the teeth line up with the indents in the wax and ask the patient to hold it in place with both thumbs.
6. Loosen the nuts on the transfer jig, attach to the frame of the facebow and feed the hole on nut 2 over the prong making sure you can see the number 1 and number 2 and they are the right way up.
7. A nurse / assistant should guide the earpieces firmly into the patient's ears pulling slightly forward as they go in.
8. Raise the pointer until it is level with the mark made previously on the nose.
9. Ensure the facebow is parallel with the interpupillary line.
10. Tighten nut 1 then nut 2 firmly.
11. Disassemble the transfer jig from the ear bow and disinfect to send to the lab.
