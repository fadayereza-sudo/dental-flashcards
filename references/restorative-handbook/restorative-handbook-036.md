## Mepivacaine hydrochloride 3% plain - Scandonest (Septodont)

1 cartridge (2.2ml) contains: 66 mg of mepivacaine.
1ml contains: 30 mg of mepivacaine.

- **Indication:** When the use of vasoconstrictor is contraindicated (i.e. patients with severe hypertension or unstable cardiac rhythm).
- **Dose:** Adults: Suggested maximum dose: 4.5 cartridges of 2.2ml in a healthy 70Kg adult. For a healthy adult of 70 kg, do not exceed 4.4 mg/kg (0.15 ml/kg) of mepivacaine with an absolute dose of 300 mg per session. Children (over 4 years of age): Average 0.75 mg/kg of mepivacaine (0.025 ml/kg). Do not exceed 3 mg/kg of mepivacaine (0.1 ml/kg).
- **Length of anaesthesia:** Shortest duration when compared to the other solutions (around 30 minutes).
- **Pregnancy and lactation:** Use with caution during pregnancy or breast-feeding.
- **Warnings and precautions:** Use with care in patients with heart, liver and/or kidney problems.
- **Contraindications:** Allergy to ingredients of this medicine.
