## Dentures

If the patient's complaint is associated with dentures, take a complete history to ensure problems are identified and addressed during construction of the new ones. Please refer to 'Prosthetics: History, Exam and Treatment Plan' for examples of certain aspects of denture examination.

Comment upon:

1. Type of dentures
2. Partially / completely edentulous
3. Saliva: Normal or Xerostomia?
4. Ridges: Firm, flabby, bone irregularities, extensive resorption (atrophic)
5. Cleanliness of existing denture
6. Nature of support: Tooth / mucosa / tooth-mucosa supported
7. Stability (good / adequate / poor); Reasons for lack of stability: Flabby ridge, unsupported ridge, extensive resorption; inadequate muscle control
8. Retention (good / adequate / poor); Reasons for lack of retention: Under or overextension of periphery, position of post dam, adaptation of fitting surface
