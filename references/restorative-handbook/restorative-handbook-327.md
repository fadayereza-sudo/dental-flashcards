**Lower alone**

- Check fit – no discomfort, no over extension, stable
- **Measure RVD** – patient upright, neck straight, at rest, lips closed, no tone in facial muscles
- **Adjust for tongue space** – posterior teeth should be directly over the ridge, should be in neutral zone (i.e. not being displaced)

**Upper and lower rims in together**

- **Retruded position** – Hold lower rim in place whilst guiding jaw into retruded position. Asking patient to feel back of upper record block with tip of tongue can help.
- **Adjust for simultaneous contact** – Watch during closure that heels of rims don't contact. Feeling the rim as it closes, feel which side meets first. Adjust as necessary until both sides meet simultaneously.
- **Meet at correct OVD** – Take 3mm away from RVD reading. Add or remove wax from lower using hotplate.
- **Mark on centre line** – Look straight at patient, not from the side, and mark line on upper rim coincident with centre of patient's face using wax knife.
- **Score in premolar region** – Use wax knife to score a line between upper and lower rims, to use as check mark later.
- **Notches in upper and lower rims** – To aid repositioning of blu-mousse if rims come apart later.
- **Apply adhesive** – Remove from mouth and apply silicone adhesive to upper and lower rims. Replace in mouth, try to keep dry.
- **Blu mousse** – Squirt blu-mousse over and behind pillar of lower, guide patient into retruded contact position, keeping lower rim held firmly down and hold there till it sets.
- **Seal blocks together** – Remove from mouth. If not firmly sealed by blu-mousse use small amount of melted wax to seal rims together.
