Specify the material to be used for the record block bases:

a) **Light-cured acrylic resin:** For rigidity.

b) **Shellac:** Specifically indicated where the inter-ridge distance is small and where the position of the post dam on the upper working impression is uncertain. Outline on the cast the extent of the shellac especially where inter-ridge height is limited.

c) **Heat-cured clear acrylic resin:** In exceptional cases where you require optimum retention and stability. This base is valuable when the prognosis for retention and stability is poor.
