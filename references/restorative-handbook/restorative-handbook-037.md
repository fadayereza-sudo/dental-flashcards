## Local anaesthesia techniques – Maxilla

For restorative procedures in maxillary teeth such as preparations for indirect and direct restorations, a buccal infiltration to the tooth to be treated is often sufficient to achieve a good level of anaesthesia. Consider a palatal infiltration/block in cases where rubber dam clamp is likely to traumatise the palatal soft tissue or for endodontics when treating 1st premolars and 1st molars, especially in cases of irreversible pulpitis. For extractions both buccal and palatal injections are always required.
