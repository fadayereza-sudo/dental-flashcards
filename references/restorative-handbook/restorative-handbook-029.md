## Corrective Therapy

Definitive treatment should not be provided until stabilization of active disease has been demonstrably achieved. Bear in mind that if definitive treatment is provided before achievement of good OH for example we might be accelerating the patient's loss of remaining teeth!

- Revisit non-responding periodontal sites
- Complete endodontic treatment
- Permanent restoration where temporary ones have been placed

## Advanced treatment

- Occlusal reorganisation
- Crown and bridge work
- Aesthetic or cosmetic treatment (place definitive restorations/crowns with rest seats and undercuts as required for denture)
- Surgical periodontology
- Definitive acrylic or metal-based partial dentures
