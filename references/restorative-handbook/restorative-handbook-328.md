- **Choose shade** – Using shade guide, match to existing dentures or choose shade appropriate to age of patient and patient's wishes.
- **Choose mould** – Three basic shapes: rounded, tapering and square. Can use photographs of patient when they had teeth if no existing dentures. Size based upon intended position of canines.
