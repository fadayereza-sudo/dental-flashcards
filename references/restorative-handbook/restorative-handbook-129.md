## Amalgam — material and indications

Permanent restorative material.

### Indications

- Large posterior load bearing cavities
- Patients with high risk of caries
- Subgingival margins
- Moisture control is difficult
- Cores for crowns
- Posterior intra canal dowel preps (Nayyar technique)

### Capsules

- Grey plunger capsules (20g alloy, 20g mercury) for small restorations
- Green plunger capsules (alloy 30g, mercury 30g) for larger restorations

### Working times

- Mixing time 5"
- Condensing time 4'30"
- Carving time 5'30"

### Composition

The dental amalgam used on clinic is a high copper alloy with decreased gamma 2 phase (the phase responsible for increased corrosion, creep and ditching). Sealable jars are located in each cubicle for disposal of surplus amalgam, capsules and contaminated dappens pots.
