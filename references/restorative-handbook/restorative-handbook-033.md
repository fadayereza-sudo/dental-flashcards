## Lidocaine hydrochloride 2% and adrenaline 1:80,000 - Lignospan Special (Septodont)

1 cartridge (2.2ml) contains: 44 mg of lidocaine and 27.5 μg of adrenaline.
1ml contains: 20 mg of lidocaine and 12.5 μg of adrenaline.

- **Indication:** Local anaesthesia for routine dental procedures by infiltration or nerve block injection.
- **Dose:** Adults: Suggested maximum dose: 4.5 cartridges of 2.2ml in a healthy 70Kg adult. Do not exceed 4.4 mg/kg (0.22 ml/kg) with an absolute dose of 200 mg of lidocaine per session and 200 µg of adrenaline whatever the lowest. Children (over 4 years of age): Average 20 to 30 mg lidocaine per session. Dose may be calculated: Child's weight (in Kg) x 1.33. Do not exceed 2.2 mg/Kg.
- **Length of anaesthesia:** Around 1.5 to 2.5 hours.
- **Pregnancy and lactation:** Crosses the placenta but not known to be harmful in animal studies - use if benefit outweighs risk. Present in milk but amount too small to be harmful.
- **Warnings and precautions:** Use with care in patients with: Liver problems; With heart problems (high blood pressure, coronary or valvular cardiac disease, heart rhythm disorders); Diabetes mellitus; or who are: Debilitated; Elderly; Acutely ill.
- **Contraindications:** Allergy to ingredients of this medicine. Patients: Suffering from porphyria; Severe hypertension; Recent MI; Epileptics; or who are taking or have taken guanethidine or related medicines.
