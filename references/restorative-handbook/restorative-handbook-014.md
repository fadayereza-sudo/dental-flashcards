## Tenderness to percussion (TTP)

In vertical plane: Will provide information about the extension of pulpal infection into the periapical region of the tooth. In horizontal plane: Could be a sign of periodontal abscess.

- Tap tooth with mirror handle in vertical or horizontal plane. Record on patient's notes if this triggered pain or not.

## Palpation of vestibule

Will provide information about the extension of pulpal infection into the periapical region of the tooth, swelling may be present.

- Palpate with index finger the buccal vestibule of the tooth/teeth checked. Record if tenderness is felt by patient.

## Fibre-optic transillumination (FOTI)

Useful to evaluate anterior and/or posterior interproximal caries also as adjunct to tooth sleuth testing when trying to locate cracks.

- Shine curing light to different surfaces of tooth, record on patient's notes visible cracks and shadows (possible caries).

## Tooth sleuth test

Will provide information whether any part(s) of the tooth is flexing as a result of a crack.

- Tooth sleuth is available from dispensary.
- Position tooth sleuth head over a cusp of the tooth with a suspected crack.
- Ask patient to bite for a few seconds then to open mouth, if pain triggered on release of bite write down the tooth number and cusp involved. Repeat this for each individual cusp.
