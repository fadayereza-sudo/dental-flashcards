## Viral infections

For Severe herpetic stomatitis and Primary herpetic gingivostomatitis drug therapy refer to the BNF.

### Herpes labialis (cold sore)

**Aciclovir cream, 5%**
- Apply 5 times daily for 5 to 10 days
- One 2g tube
- Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes
- Best prescribed asap in the prodromal phase prior to the actual eruption of the vesicles, after it there is limited benefit in prescribing it, although it may offer a slight improvement in the course of the illness
