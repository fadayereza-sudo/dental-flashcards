## Intra-oral examination

### Soft tissues

Besides general notes regarding soft tissues' health status, notes must be made regarding presence of lesions and where appropriate, referral for photographs and further investigations.

Tissues to be examined:

- Labial mucosa
- Buccal mucosa (cheek biting? sign of active bruxism)
- Tongue (tongue scalloping? sign of active bruxism)
- Floor of mouth and lingual sulci
- Hard and soft palate (ask patient to say 'Aahhh' to allow examination of the pharyngeal wall and the tonsils)

### Soft tissue lesion characteristics to record

- **Location:** Anatomic site(s)
- **Size:** Length and width
- **Colour:** White (leukoplakia), red (erythroplakia), speckled (erythroleukoplakia), homogeneous vs. nonhomogeneous
- **Outline:** Discrete vs. diffuse
- **Texture:** Smooth, flat, raised, dome shaped, granular, verrucous, ulcerated, indurated
- **Time:** How long this has been present?

Unexplained ulcers of more than three weeks' duration warrants an urgent specialist review.
