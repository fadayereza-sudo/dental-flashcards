Before sending any work to the laboratory it should be disinfected in the "Perform" bath in the unit for 10 minutes prior to rinsing and appropriate storage for transit to the lab. If there has been any blood contamination of any objects they should be thoroughly rinsed prior to immersion in the perform bath.
