## Pain History

If PAIN present, pain history may be assisted using the SOCRATES acronym:

- **S = Site:** Can the patient determine the tooth/area causing the pain?
- **O = Onset:** When did the pain start? Spontaneous or provoked by stimuli (hot, cold or sweets)?
- **C = Character:** Sharp (usually sign of decay, crack/fracture...). Dull, throbbing (could be sign of bacterial infection to periodontal/periapical tissues).
- **R = Radiation:** Does the pain spread?
- **A = Associated factors:** Swelling, sinus or systemic involvement (fever). Bad breath? Bad taste?
- **T = Timing:** How long does the pain last (seconds, minutes or hours)? Worse at any particular time of day? Does it wake you up at night?
- **E = Exacerbating/relieving factors:** Painkillers, are they effective?
- **S = Severity:** Affecting quality of life? On a scale of 1 to 10?
