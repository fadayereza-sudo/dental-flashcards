There are many factors that could influence the type of crown provided for a patient, including the patient's desires, whether the crowned tooth will act as an abutment, the presence of parafunctional habits and the availability of tooth height.

For anterior teeth Emax crowns are suitable in most cases.

For molars, monolithic zirconia is suitable in most cases.

An MCC or FVC may be the preferred option if the crown will be an abutment for a bridge or cobalt / chrome partial denture or for patients with parafunctional habits.
