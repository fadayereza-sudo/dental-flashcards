## Dentine hypersensitivity

Dentine hypersensitivity is caused by exposed dentine resulting in pain in response to an external stimulus (touch and/or hot and/or cold foods/drinks).

### Signs and symptoms

- Pain (sharp of short duration provoked by stimulus)
- Exposed root surface as a result of gingival recession

### Management

- Check for other causes of dental pain (e.g. reversible pulpitis)
- Apply fluoride varnish or dentine bonging agent to exposed dentine
- Advise the patient to avoid acidic foods/drinks and to apply small amount of desensitising toothpaste to the affected area with a finger
