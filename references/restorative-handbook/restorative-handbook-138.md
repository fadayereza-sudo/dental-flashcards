## Etch

- Etch both enamel and dentine for 15s with 37.5% Phosphoric acid gel (Total-etch technique: both enamel and dentine are etched simultaneously prior to application of bonding agent).
- Rinse thoroughly for 15 - 20s.

## Bond

- Dry lightly to remove excessive moisture but do not desiccate (5s).
- Apply 'OptiBond Solo Plus Total-Etch adhesive' to both enamel and dentine surfaces with applicator tip for 15s, using light brushing motion (Optibond contains both primer + adhesive and it is not self-etching, the wording 'Total-Etch' refers to the etching technique).
- Gently thin with a stream of air for 3s.
- Light cure for 20s.

## Self etching adhesives

Scotchbond Universal adhesive can be used in a self-etching technique. Selective etching of enamel is indicated when trying to bond to enamel that has not been prepared.
