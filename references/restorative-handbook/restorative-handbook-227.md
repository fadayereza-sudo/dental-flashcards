Where aesthetics allows, gold margins can be employed as a means of further preserving tooth tissue by creating a chamfer instead of a shoulder, the technician will wax up a gold substructure and incorporate the porcelain once there is adequate room available. This is particularly useful recession cases. For the patient there is a visible gold margin to the crown, but fit and cleansibility are superior.
