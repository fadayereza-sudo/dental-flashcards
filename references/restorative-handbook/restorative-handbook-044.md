## Long buccal block

- **Numbs:** Soft tissues and periosteum buccal to the mandibular molars.
- **Landmarks:** Mandibular molars and the mucobuccal fold.
- **Injection site:** Distal and buccal to the most distal molar in the arch on the anterior border of the ramus.
- **Dose:** No more than ½ cartridge.
