## Pre-treatment baseline indices and records

Based on your diagnosis collect additional information as appropriate:

- Study models
- Preoperative clinical photographs
- Behaviour management
- Diet analysis
- For diagnosis of gingivitis (BPE of 1 and 2):
  - Plaque free scores (PFS), as often as required
  - Marginal gingival bleeding free scores (MBFS)
- For diagnosis of periodontitis:
  - Plaque free scores (PFS), as often as required
  - In case of BPE of 3:
    - Record a 6 point pocket chart (probing pocket depth (PPD)) in that sextant only as well as Bleeding on probing (BOP) only post-initial therapy (OHI and risk factor control)
  - In case of BPE of 4:
    - Full mouth 6 point pocket chart (probing pocket depth (PPD)) prior to initial therapy
    - Bleeding on probing (BOP)
  - Suppuration, furcation involvement, mobility and recession
- Further radiographic examinations (it is assumed that all required radiographs were already taken during history and examination, but if needs be further investigations should be carried out at this stage)
