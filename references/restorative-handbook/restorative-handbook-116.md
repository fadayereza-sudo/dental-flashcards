## Fungal infections

For Acute erythematous candidiasis or Chronic hyperplastic candidiasis drug therapy refer to the BNF.

### Angular chelitis

**Miconazole oral gel, 24 mg/ml**
- Apply 2.5ml 2 times daily, after meals for 5 to 7 days
- One 80g tube
- Apply to corners of mouth
- Continue treatment for at least 7 days after lesion or symptoms have disappeared

Miconazole: do not prescribe for patients taking warfarin as interaction increases bleeding risk. Avoid (GP should be consulted) for patients taking statins as it may increase the risk of side effects such as liver and kidney damage. Avoid in pregnancy and breast feeding.
