**Check OVD**

- Insert lower, measure RVD
- Insert upper, measure OVD
- Assess freeway space
- If minor problem, review after 1 week, but if significant discrepancy may require re-make

**Speech check**

- Appearance of patient's lips when in occlusion
- Speech (get patient to count from 60-70). If difficulty noticed forming the letter 's' then reassess OVD
