Extra-oral examination includes assessment of skeletal classification, biological age, facial habits, appearance, occlusal vertical dimension. Look for evidence of over closure which occurs with inadequate OVD and watch the patient as they are speaking to gauge some insight into the sufficiency of the freeway space.

Assess appearance as affected by the dentures, e.g. facial and lip support, naso-labial angle, creasing and folds at the corners of the mouth, and midlines.
