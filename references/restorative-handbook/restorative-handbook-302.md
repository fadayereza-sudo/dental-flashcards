**Creation of guide surfaces**

Guide surfaces can dramatically increase the retention and stability of a partial denture. Ideally they should be parallel to the path of insertion, but not necessarily parallel to the path of natural displacement. Guide surfaces are best if they have greater than 3mm height, should wrap around the proximal surface of the tooth and be kept wholly within enamel.
