## Oral Hygiene

- Poor / Fair / Good
- Presence or absence of plaque and/or calculus?
- Generalized or in localized areas?
- Bleeding?

## Recession

Williams Probe, measured from CEJ to gingival margin: 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10mm (Attachment loss: recession + pocket depth).

Used for checking pocket depths and recession.

## Mobility

Measured after the tooth is pressed gently bucco-lingually between the mirror handle and digit:

- **Grade I:** Mobility less than 1mm in a horizontal direction
- **Grade II:** Mobility of over 1mm in a horizontal direction
- **Grade III:** Mobility of over 1mm in a horizontal direction plus vertical movement as well
