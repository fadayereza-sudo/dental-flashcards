The principles of effective root canal treatment remain as we always knew them to be: clean, shape, fill. However, the emphasis has changed towards a more biological approach, with a greater appreciation of the bacterial nature of the disease process and a better understanding of factors affecting outcome. Success is not achieved through obsession with the length of root filling as it appears on a radiograph. It is more dependent on thorough cleaning and shaping of the canal system and provision of a good coronal seal. Success can be defined as absence of pain and clinical signs of disease and evidence of peri-radicular health.
