## Occlusion

Make a record according to the patient's occlusion:

- Canine guidance / Group function / Premolar guidance or N/A?
- Mark this for right and left. If group function present, which teeth are involved?
- Incisal edge contacts: Protrusive Edge to edge contact (which teeth in contact?) or N/A?
- Intercuspal position (ICP): stable or unstable?
- Centre lines: co-incident / left or right shift?
- Open bite; Crossbite (anterior, posterior (unilateral, bilateral))

### Incisor relationship

- **Class I:** Lower incisors bite 2-4 mm behind the upper incisors
- **Class II div 1:** Lower incisors bite further behind the upper incisors which are proclined (overjet)
- **Class II div 2:** Lower incisors bite further behind the upper incisors which are retroclined (overbite)
- **Class III:** Lower incisor bite further forwards of the upper incisors than normal
