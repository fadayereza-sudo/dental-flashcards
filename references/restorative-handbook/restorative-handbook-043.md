## Mental block

- **Numbs:** Incisors, canine, first premolars and most of the time second premolars, also lower lip and chin skin, all on the operative side.
- **Landmarks:** Mandibular premolars, mucobuccal fold and mental foramen.
- **Injection site:** Mucobuccal fold just anterior to the mental foramen where the mental nerve enter to join the incisive nerve. Radiographs will easily help locate the mental foramen.
- **Dose:** No more than ⅔ of a cartridge.
