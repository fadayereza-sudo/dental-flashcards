Monolithic zirconia is a recent addition to the list of crowns that we can provide. They are an alternative crown to the FVC for patients that wish for a metal-free restoration. Preparation is comparable to that for an FVC, and is not overly destructive.

**Preparation:**

- 0.3 to 0.5 mm chamfer
- 1 – 1.5 mm occlusal reduction
- 6-8° taper
- All angles rounded
