## Diagnosis

All diagnoses to be listed which should form the rationale for the ensuing treatment plan. Report findings in this order:

1. Extra-oral
2. Intra-oral
3. Periodontal status
4. Caries
5. Tooth wear
6. Endo
7. Occlusal problems
8. Failing bridge work
9. Missing teeth causing aesthetic or functional problems

Always discuss your findings with your tutor and remember, no diagnosis, no treatment!
