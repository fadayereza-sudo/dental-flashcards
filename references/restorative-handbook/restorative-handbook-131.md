## Equipment required & step-by-step guide (amalgam restoration)

### Equipment required

From dispensary: LA syringe and needle, high/slow speed handpiece, No.7 high speed diamond bur, Rose head slow speed bur, amalgam carrier, amalgam capsule, dappens pot.
From blue trolley: local anaesthetic cartridge, lining material – RMGIC (Fuji Lining), bonding material – Panavia or RMGIC (Fuji Lining), wooden wedges.
In surgery: topical anaesthetic, cons kit, matrix band, articulating paper.

### Process

1. Administer topical and local anaesthetic.
2. Moisture control (rubber dam or cotton rolls and appropriate suction in selected cases).
3. Complete caries removal as per guidance in 'Caries Removal' section.
4. Check if general guidelines for cavity preparation for amalgam restorations were met:
   - At least 2mm deep for adequate strength
   - Wide enough to enable use of the smallest packer
   - Cavo-surface angles of > 90°
   - Amalgam margin angles > 70°
   - No unsupported enamel
   - Rounded internal angles
5. Check the cavity design for retention:
   - Is there sufficient undercut to retain amalgam?
   - Are additional grooves or cavity wall modifications required to improve retention or is a bonded restoration indicated?
6. Check the cavity design for resistance:
   - Is there a satisfactory occlusal key or do the cavity design provides resistance form already?
   - If not, can features such as dovetails and grooves be added to aid this, or is a bonded restoration indicated?
   - Is the floor of any box flat to offer vertical resistance under occlusal loading?
7. If the cavity extends interproximally:
   - Width should just clear contact area
   - Bottom of box must be below contact point
   - The gingival wall should be level, not slopping
   - Matrix band must be placed over the tooth ensuring it sits beyond all cervical cavity margins, then apply wedge and burnish matrix band to create contact point
8. Assess need for cavity liners:
   - If the cavity is shallow to moderate no liner is required
   - Deep: apply RMGIC liner (Fuji Lining)
   - Pinpoint pulpal exposure: apply setting Ca(OH)2 in the area then RMGIC liner (Fuji Lining)
   - Keep lining materials away from enamel
9. If cavity is considered unretentive a thin layer of dual-cure resin cement (RMGIC liner, Panavia or Rely-X) should be placed over all walls, on dentine but not over enamel.
10. Mix amalgam, dispense capsule content in a Dappens pot, load the amalgam carrier and place amalgam into the cavity.
11. Firmly compact the amalgam into the deepest parts of the cavity first (initial packer has to fit into the cavity and interproximal boxes floor).
12. Once the cavity is full to the margins (use adjacent marginal ridges as a guide for amount of amalgam likely required in class II restorations), begin shaping. Clear the inner periphery of matrix bands with a sharp probe, remove the band then begin occlusal contouring with a Ward's Carver, burnisher.
13. Check occlusion and finish.
