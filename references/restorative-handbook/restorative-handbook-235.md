Opposing arch alginate impressions are required. Where the Inter Cuspal Position (ICP) requires recording then a jaw registration material such as Blu Mousse can be used (in conjunction with record rims where required). Where there is a clear ICP and hand held models could be located, then simply Blu Mousse over the preparation to assist location is all that is required. If recording the retruded contact position Moyco Hard Pink Beauty Wax is the material of choice. If current study casts are available the opposing arch impression can obviously be omitted. Where multiple crowns or groups of crowns are being undertaken simultaneously, appropriate locating registrations for articulation of the working cast will be required.
