These have superseded Porcelain Jacket Crowns (PJC's) unless previous PJC's are being replaced, like with like.

Similar to veneer preparations, the dentine bonded crown preparation is minimal. The resulting crown is weak until cemented.

The preparation has a chamfered margin on all aspects and is similar to that which might result from the preparation of an anterior tooth for a full veneer gold crown. The preparation should comprise mainly sound tooth tissue (enamel and dentine) with a limited proportion of composite resin or glass ionomer build up being acceptable.

This type of crown is cemented using a resin luting system similar to that which would be used for ceramic veneers.

**Criteria for preparation:**

- Incisal reduction (1.5 - 2mm)
- Well defined recordable margins (chamfer):
  - Buccal / labial reduction (0.5 - 0.7mm), least in cervical region, follow anatomical form
  - Palatal reduction (0.5 - 0.7mm), follow anatomical form
- Interproximal reduction
- 10 - 15° taper
- Supragingival margins (0.5mm) if possible (consider aesthetic issues)
- No undercuts
- Smooth surface finish
- No iatrogenic damage to adjacent teeth
