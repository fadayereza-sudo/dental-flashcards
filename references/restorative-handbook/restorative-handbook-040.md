## Greater palatine block

- **Numbs:** Posterior portion of the hard palate both hard and soft tissues distal to the mesial aspect of the right and left first premolars.
- **Landmarks:** Greater palatine foramen.
- **Injection site:** Soft tissue just anterior to the depression created by the greater palatine foramen, usually distal to the upper 2nd molar but it can be more anterior.
- **Dose:** Up to ½ cartridge.
