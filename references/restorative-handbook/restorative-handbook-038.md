## Maxillary buccal infiltration

- **Numbs:** Pulp, buccal soft tissue and bone of the tooth being anaesthetized.
- **Landmarks:** Mucobuccal fold parallel to the tooth root apex close to the bone.
- **Injection site:** At the height of the mucobuccal fold parallel to the long axis of the tooth to be anaesthetized, at or above the apex of the tooth.
- **Dose:** Up to ½ cartridge.
