## Clamps

Clamps from different companies may have different names, most of the available ones in our clinics are named as below. If names are not the same, their shapes and indications are still the same as shown.

All clamps should be adequately flossed prior to placement in the mouth and the floss should drape from the mouth at all times. There have been several incidents of clamps fracturing during use.

| Clamp | Indication |
|---|---|
| K | General winged molar clamp |
| EW | Incisors, canines and premolars |
| G | Small molars, pre-molars and canines |
| JW | Molars with deep gingival fit |
| L | Incisors, canines and premolars with limited gingival retention |
| PW | Large, bland molar clamp |
| Butterfly | Incisors, canines and premolars |
