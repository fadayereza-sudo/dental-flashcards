**Denture Hygiene and advice**

- Highlight limitations of dentures
- Verbal and written instructions
- Soft diet for the first few days
- Friends and relatives may notice changes — this is normal, particularly if significant changes have been made
- Hyper-salivation and speech problems may occur but should settle after a few days
- If soreness develops which is minor, patient should persist with denture until review stage
- If soreness is extensive, remove dentures, replace with old dentures, and insert new dentures a few hours before review
- Remove dentures before sleep, with exception being the first 2 weeks
- Use soap and water to clean dentures with a soft brush
- Always follow manufacturer's instructions if denture cleansers are used
- Review in 1 week
- Keep laboratory prescription in notes
- Give Medical Devices Statement to patient and copy in notes with statement that this has been carried out
