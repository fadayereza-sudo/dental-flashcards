## Disease stabilisation

The order of treatment can be modified depending on the patient's concerns or symptoms and disease progression rate.

- **Prevention** (Preventive strategies should be the priority in this stage), most common preventive measures are:
  - Oral hygiene instruction
  - Fluoride supplements (toothpaste, mouth rinse, gels, varnish)
  - Smoking cessation
  - Individual diet counselling
  - Fissure sealants
- Extraction of hopeless teeth
- Removal of plaque retaining factors (Supragingival calculus, overhanging restorations)
- Non-surgical periodontal therapy
- Endodontic stabilisation – pulp extirpation and dressing
- Caries stabilisation – permanent or temporary restorations (in cases where caries is extensive, deep or gingival health is too poor for adequate moisture control)
- Provisional prostheses
