## Irreversible pulpitis (inflamed pulp which will eventually progress to necrosis)

- **C/O Pain:** Can be spontaneous, may be provoked by hot / cold. Throbbing pain that may linger. Poorly localised. May disturb sleep.
- **Special tests:** May be TTP. Heat can elicit pain.
- **Clinically:** Deep caries / large restoration / crown.
- **Radiograph:** Deep caries, may see early PA changes.
- **Treatment:** RCT / extraction.
