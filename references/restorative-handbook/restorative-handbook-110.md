## Drug prescribing for dentistry

The information below will help you when writing prescriptions for some routinely prescribed fluoride containing products, analgesics, antibiotics, antifungals and antivirals for dentistry for adult patients. The tables contain the drug name, instructions of use, quantity required for the course of treatment and recommendations to be given to the patients.

Drug regimens and brief description of pathologies were all taken from the British National Formulary on September/2016 and the document 'Drug Prescribing for Dentistry' published by SDCEP (2016). Please refer to these for more up to date information and other drug options, as well as doses for children and elderly patients. Note: Some doses and first choice medication are different between these two documents, although both recommendations are accepted, drug therapy should be assessed on an individual basis.

Prior to prescribing, always discuss with your tutor the necessity of drug therapy and the patient's medical history to reduce the risk of allergic reactions and drug interactions.
