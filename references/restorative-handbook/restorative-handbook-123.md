## Impressions for study models

Articulators are essential for allowing the technician to model the jaw movements and tooth contacts for the patient, however they can only be as good as the records we send to the lab, including impressions, interocclusal records and facebow records.

These are usually taken using alginate, which is only not the most dimensionally stable material and must be used in the most effective way.

When taking an impression:

- Dry the teeth with gauze just prior to the impression
- Scoop some alginate onto your index finger and lightly wipe into the fissures
- Keep steady pressure on the impression whilst the alginate is setting
- Check the impression for air blows in the fissures and retake if necessary
- Never let the impression dry out, after disinfection wrap in wet gauze & seal in a plastic bag
