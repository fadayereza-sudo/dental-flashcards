## Referral (Ref)

Where the patient has been referred from:

- Consultant / GDP / Self-referral

This is very important, you MUST always know what the origin of the patient was. It is not sufficient to state which waiting list they came from.

Consultant – any patients coming through consultant clinics should have a plan that must be followed. Any thoughts of deviating from that plan MUST be discussed with the consultant in charge of the patient.

GDP – Usually these patients will first have seen a consultant. If we find treatment that we think is required that was not what the GDP referred the patient for we must check with the GDP via letter who should complete that treatment. We can offer to do it but we must not continue until the GDP has agreed. Following treatment a letter should go to the GDP informing them of what we have done and what maintenance is required.
