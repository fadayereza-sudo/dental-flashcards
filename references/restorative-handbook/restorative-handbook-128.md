## Stepwise excavation technique

1. Place rubber dam, remove infected dentine, leave affected dentine, ensure ADJ is caries free throughout cavity.
2. Place layer of setting calcium hydroxide over deep affected dentine and restore cavity with RMGIC (e.g. Fuji II LC).
3. Re-enter after a few weeks (tooth should be symptomless).
4. Restore with 'closed sandwich technique' (leave RMGIC as base over pulpal/axial wall) or 'open sandwich technique' if deep interproximal cavity (leave RMGIC restoration from base of proximal cavity up to the level of the ADJ and as base). Then, in both cases, cut back to a sufficient depth for a permanent restoration (at least 2mm for composite or amalgam).
