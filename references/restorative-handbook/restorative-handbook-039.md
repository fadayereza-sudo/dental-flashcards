## Naso palatine block

- **Numbs:** Anterior portion of the hard palate both hard and soft tissues from the canines and medial to the midline.
- **Landmarks:** Central incisors, incisive papilla and incisive foramen.
- **Injection site:** Soft tissue just lateral to the incisive papilla, 1cm palatal to the maxillary central incisors.
- **Dose:** Until tissue blanch.
