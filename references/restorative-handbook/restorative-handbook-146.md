## Pulpal exposure

If the exposure is small and the tooth was previously symptomless then rubber dam should be placed immediately before fetching the tutor. Setting calcium hydroxide should be placed directly over the exposure site and sealed in with RMGIC (Fuji liner).

## Liners

Liners are not routinely used for composite or GIC restorations. For Amalgam restorations an RMGIC liner (Fuji liner) may be used if the cavity is deep.

### Fuji Lining (RMGIC)

**Indications:**
- Base / liner in prepared cavities
- Sealing pulpal floor and gutta percha in endo (markedly reduces microleakage)
- For bonding amalgam

**Notes:**
- Conditioning is optional (for optimum bond GC Cavity Conditioner for 10s, wash 20")
- Light cure for 20"
- Should not be placed over direct pulp exposure
- Slightly cytotoxic and antibacterial
- Times: mixing 10", working 2'15"

### Dycal (setting calcium hydroxide)

**Indications:**
- Vital pulpal exposure

**Notes:**
- Does not reduce microleakage and it is cytotoxic
- Needs to be sealed in with another material such as RMGIC (Fuji lining) before placement of restoration
- Times: mixing 10", working 2'20", setting 2'30" to 3'30"

### Hypocal (non-setting calcium hydroxide)

**Indications:**
- Inter-visit medicament of choice for RCT temporary obturation material
- Pulpal exposure or suspected exposure

**Notes:**
- Bacteriocidal, 12.5 pH allows pulpal recovery if exposure has occurred
- No light-curing, no conditioning
