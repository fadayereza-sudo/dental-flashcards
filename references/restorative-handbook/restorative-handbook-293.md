**A guide to the choice of special trays**

**Maxillary Arch — Edentulous**

| Condition | Flabby ridge or marked bony undercut | Minimal/No undercut | Partially dentate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tray Type | Close fitting, no perforations | Spaced, lots of perforations | Spaced, perforated |
| Impression material | Light bodied silicone | Medium bodied silicone or alginate | Alginate if few standing teeth or teeth with recession present; Medium bodied silicone otherwise |

**Mandibular Arch**

| Condition | Marked bony undercut | Minimal/No undercut | Partially dentate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tray Type | Close fitting, no perforations | Spaced, perforated | Spaced over teeth, close fitting over free end saddle regions; Perforated where teeth are only, no perforations over saddles |
| Impression material | Light/medium bodied silicone | Alginate or medium bodied silicone | Alginate if few standing teeth or teeth with recession present; Medium bodied silicone otherwise |
