## Inferior alveolar block

- **Numbs:** Teeth in the quadrant, tongue and lingual soft tissues, buccal soft tissues from mental nerve to midline.
- **Landmarks:** We are aiming for the mandibular foramen. Ask the patient to open wide and locate the anterior border of the ramus, the coronoid notch and slide your thumb laterally pulling the soft tissue taut. Now you should be able to see the pterygomandibular raphe (PMR), then imagine that the distance between this and the coronoid notch (CN) is divided in 4.
- **Injection site and technique:** Come with the syringe from the premolars/canine region on the opposite side, placing the needle at the limit between the 1st and 2nd quarter distance between the PMR and CN at a height of about 1 to 1.5cm above the occlusal plane. Advance needle slowly about 2.5cm (1cm of needle will still be outside the tissues) you should hit bone at this stage, withdraw slightly, aspirate, if negative inject the anaesthetic solution.
  - If you hit bone too soon, withdraw the needle without getting out of the tissues and reduce the angulation (bring the syringe between canine and incisors region) and advance again.
  - If you don't hit bone at all, do the same but increase the angulation (bring the syringe between premolar and molar region) and advance again.
- **Dose:** Around 1 cartridge.
