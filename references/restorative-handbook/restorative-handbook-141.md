## Occlusion

Check with articulating paper and confirm with patient if bite feels ok.

- Anterior tooth: Check ICP, protrusion and lateral guidance.
- Posterior tooth: Check ICP, also excursive movements if this tooth is in group function.

## Core build up

Composite may be used as a core material but it is suggested that any impressions are delayed for 1 week following placement. This will allow hygroscopic expansion to occur before preparation rather than during the period that the crown is being constructed.

## Bulk fill composite

We have the bulk fill composite (Dentsply SDR) into the armamentarium of the dental students. This is to be used as an alternative to a layering technique for occlusal and approximal posterior restorations.
