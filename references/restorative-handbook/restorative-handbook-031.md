## Ultra safety plus syringes

This is a sterile, single use, aspirating syringe system, specially designed to prevent needle stick injuries (no recapping is necessary). It also comes with a bevel indicator to assist in orientating the bevel to the bone.

There are short and long needles (blue cap = 2.5cm and yellow cap = 3.5cm).

Long needles are only required for inferior alveolar blocks, while short needles are suitable for all other techniques described in the handbook.

Assembly:

1. Place cartridge inside part A.
2. Attach A over B until fully pushed on.
3. Slide C back fully until click is heard.
4. After using, slide C to cover needle until hear first click. At this point can re-use or slide further for second click prior to disposal.

Please note: White handles are single use, black handles should be sterilised for repeated use.
