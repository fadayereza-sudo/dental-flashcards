Lithium disilicate ceramic combines great aesthetics and high strength in a metal free material, selected cases could really benefit from this technique. Students wishing to provide e.max crowns, please discuss the case with a clinical tutor.

After tooth preparation, a silicone impression is obtained and the tooth is temporised. Once a plaster model is produced from this impression, it should be scanned and the e.max restoration milled in our dental laboratory.
