## Local anaesthesia techniques – Mandible

For mandibular teeth all restorative procedures, endodontics included, can be carried out with an inferior alveolar block (which anaesthetises the distribution of the inferior alveolar and lingual nerves to the operating side), a buccal infiltration or long buccal block may be required if either the rubber dam clamp or the procedure itself is likely to traumatise the soft tissues buccal to the molar teeth. For molar extractions ID block + buccal infiltration are always required. A mental block will anaesthetise incisors, canine, 1st premolar and sometimes 2nd premolar on the operative side as well as the buccal soft tissue to these teeth. Buccal (and, if necessary, lingual) infiltrations can also be used when treating only lower anteriors.
