## Bonding system

### Optibond Solo Plus

**Indications:**
- As liner under composite restorations (reduces microleakage)

**Notes:**
- Should not be placed over direct pulp exposure
- Slightly cytotoxic and antibacterial

## Others

### Kalzinol (zinc-oxide eugenol)

**Indications:**
- Soothing temporary filling

**Notes:**
- Mixing ratio: powder liquid ratio 5:1 (by weight)
- Times: mixing 1' to 2', working 1'30", setting 3'30" to 4'30"

### Odontopaste

**Indications:**
- Endodontic dressing of teeth with irreversible pulpitis and pulp extirpation is not possible due to failure of anaesthetic

**Notes:**
- It should be used as a dressing only and not as a permanent lining underneath restorations
- Should not be used as a direct pulp cap
