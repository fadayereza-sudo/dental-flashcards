## Acute Apical Abscess

- **C/O Pain:** Intense throbbing pain. Intra or extra oral swelling. May feel unwell.
- **Special tests:** TTP present. Negative sensitivity test.
- **Clinically:** Tooth may be mobile. Fluctuant swelling present.
- **Radiograph:** May appear normal if rapid onset, may see periapical area.
- **Treatment:** Drainage of pus. Antibiotics if systemic involvement. Followed by RCT / extract.
