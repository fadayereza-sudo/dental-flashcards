## Chronic Periapical Abscess

- **C/O Pain:** Usually symptom free.
- **Special tests:** May feel different on percussion. -ve sensitivity tests.
- **Clinically:** Sinus may be present.
- **Radiograph:** Periapical radiolucency, loss of lamina dura.
- **Treatment:** RCT / Extract.
