## Dental anaesthetics

Lidocaine, prilocaine, articaine and mepivacaine are available at our clinics, all may be considered for routine dental procedures. Cartridges of lidocaine and articaine contain adrenaline. Use of adrenaline can be justified for most dental procedures, but it may be wise to minimize the dose for patients receiving specific medications and those with cardiovascular disease. Where adrenaline is contraindicated, consider prilocaine (with felypressin) or mepivacaine (plain solution), in doubt discuss with tutor.

Note: All cartridges available at our clinics are latex free.

Irrespective of which solution is selected, a few basic concepts apply:

- Avoid injecting into an inflamed or infected area.
- Always aspirate!! (If anaesthetic solutions are injected into a blood vessel this would cause toxic reactions).
- The lowest dose leading to efficient anaesthesia should be used, this must be determined on an individual basis (i.e. an underweight elderly patient would require smaller dosages than an average weight young adult).

For safety reasons, the maximum number of cartridges suggested to be dispensed during a single session are reduced in comparison to the manufacturer's recommendations, if higher doses are required please calculate accordingly.
