## Acute pseudomembranous candidiasis (TRUSH)

**Nystatin oral suspension, 100,000 units/ml**
- 1ml 4 times daily for 7 days after meals
- One 30ml bottle and pipette
- After meals, hold close to lesion then swallow
- Continue treatment for 48h after lesion or symptoms have disappeared

**or Fluconazole capsules, 50 mg**
- 50 to 100 mg once a day for 7 to 14 days (consult BNF for more information)
- 14 to 28 capsules

**or Miconazole oral gel, 24 mg**
- Apply 2.5ml (pea-size) 4 times daily, after meals for 5 to 7 days
- One 80g tube
- After meals, hold close to lesion then swallow
- Continue treatment for at least 7 days after lesion or symptoms have disappeared
- Dentures should be taken out at night and brushed with gel

Usually an acute infection but it may persist for months in patients receiving inhaled corticosteroids, cytotoxics or broad-spectrum antibacterials as well as in immunocompromised patients (e.g. leukaemia, other malignancies, HIV). When TRUSH is associated with corticosteroid inhalers, rinsing the mouth with water immediately after using the inhaler may avoid the problem.

Fluconazole and miconazole: do not prescribe for patients taking warfarin as interaction increases bleeding risk. Avoid (GP should be consulted) for patients taking statins as it may increase the risk of side effects such as liver and kidney damage. Avoid in pregnancy and breast feeding.

Fluconazole: for unresponsive infections or if a topical antifungal drug cannot be used or if the patient has dry mouth or is immunocompromised.
