## Glass ionomer cements

The materials which are currently available include:

### Fuji II LC (RMGIC, various shades) — Permanent and temporary restorative

**Indications:**
- Cervical lesions where moisture control is problematic or margins are in dentine
- Root caries
- Approximal lesions in anterior teeth where aesthetics are unimportant
- Liner/base under direct or indirect restorations

**Times:** mixing 10", working 3'15".

**Restoration technique:** Just prepare the cavity and apply GC Cavity Conditioner for 10" to remove the smear layer and seal the dentin tubules. Place the mixed Fuji II LC into the cavity. Light cure, trim and finish.

**Liner/Base technique:** Prepare cavity, apply GC Cavity Conditioner for 10" and rinse for 20". Place mixed Fuji II LC as a base or liner, then light cure for 20". Acid etch as needed, apply enamel bonding agent and finish the restoration with composite.

### Fuji Triage (GIC, Pink) — Temporization during endodontic treatment

**Times:** mixing 10", working 1'40", setting 2'30".

Conditioning is optional (GC Cavity Conditioner for 10", wash 20"). Wait 6' (chemically set) after activation for finishing under air water spray or 4' if light cured. 2 coats of varnish or unfilled composite resin should be applied immediately following restoration.

### ChemFil Rock (GIC, various shades) — Semi-permanent restoration in high caries rate stabilization phase

**Times:** mixing 15", working 1'30", setting 6'.

No conditioning. No light-curing. Wait 6' for finishing under air water spray.

### ChemFil Rock (GIC, Contrast white) — Temporization during endodontic treatment

**Times:** mixing 15", working 1'30", setting 6'.

Apply a coat of varnish or unfilled resin immediately following restoration.

## Shade taking

This should be performed on a clean, wet tooth before rubber dam application. The patient should be informed of the relative opacity of the initially placed cement if the restored tooth is easily visible.
