When using prefabricated posts, composite cores can be constructed either anteriorly or posteriorly and amalgam posteriorly. Glass ionomer cements and cermets (Ketac) are not strong enough to resist the tensile forces.

If composite is used as a core material, any impressions should be delayed for 1 week following placement. This will allow hygroscopic expansion to occur before preparation rather than during the period that the crown is being constructed.
