The students should try to gain breadth of experience and to undertake examples of as wide a range of different crown types as possible. However they must recognise that all crowns are destructive and most require removal of large amounts of sound tooth substance. They should therefore be encouraged to explore fully any less invasive options (e.g. composite build ups, intra coronal restorations or veneers) as part of the diagnostic and prescriptive process.
