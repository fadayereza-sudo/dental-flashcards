## Choice of articulator

In theory the more complex the articulator the better it will recreate the jaw movements, however usually we are mainly interested if a restoration is conformative in ICP. Also, there are certain circumstances where we need to ensure no lateral interferences are being created.

The bottom line is:

| Articulator selection | Case |
|---|---|
| Simple hinge | For single crowns / inlays / onlays for a patient with stable ICP and lateral guidance is not on the tooth being restored |
| Average value | For denture cases |
| Semi-adjustable (with a facebow) | For more complex restoration (bridgework, single/multiple crowns) and therapeutic splints, where recreating lateral guidance is important e.g. the guidance involves the tooth being restored |
