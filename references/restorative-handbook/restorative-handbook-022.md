## Cracked tooth syndrome

- **C/O Pain:** Depends on pulp status. May be worse on biting, classically release of bite.
- **Special tests:** May or may not be TTP. May or may not be vital. Tooth sleuth detects pain on release of biting. Transillumination with curing light may show up crack.
- **Clinically:** Crack may be visible, usually large restoration present.
- **Radiograph:** Crack may be visible.
- **Treatment:** +/- RCT depending on pulp status. Usually requires coronal protection – crown / onlay.
