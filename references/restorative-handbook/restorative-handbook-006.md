## Extra-oral examination

Here can be comments regarding:

**General** (starts even before the patient sits in the dental chair):

- Mobility, demeanour, level of awareness, speech
- Skeletal pattern (I, II, III)
- Asymmetry

**Skin and perioral tissues:**

- Any lesions?

**Lymph nodes and glands:**

- Feel for enlargement and/or tenderness
- Palpate cheeks and parotid glands
- Examine submental region (lymph nodes)
- Examine submandibular triangle (submandibular glands and lymph nodes)
- Palpate tissues of neck against thumbs and muscles (any lumps?)
- Palpate the posterior triangle

**Articulatory system:**

TMJ
- Tender to palpation? Right and/or left?
- Clicks / crepitus on opening and/or closing? Painful or painless?
- Limitation of mouth opening? (normal around 40mm)
- Deviation of the mandible when opening? If present, lasting or transient? Right or left?

Muscles of mastication (MOM)
- Tenderness to palpation?
- Muscular hypertrophy? (sign of parafunctional activity)
- Masseter: Roll the muscle between two digits
- Temporalis: Palpate anterior fibers first, then posterior
- Lateral Pterygoid: difficult palpation (intra-oral), carry out a resisted movement test instead:
  - Patient in supine position, hold patient's head firmly by placing one hand on patient's forehead
  - Ask the patient to slide their jaw towards the right hand side, teeth slightly apart
  - Holding the head firm, ask the patient to resist us trying to push their mandible back with just their mandible (check if the opposite side (left) is tender)
- Medial Pterygoid: difficult to palpate and doesn't respond to resistive movement tests
