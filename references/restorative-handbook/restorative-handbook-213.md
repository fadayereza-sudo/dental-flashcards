Non-setting calcium hydroxide (Hypocal) has a pH around 12, is anti-bacterial and proteolytic. It is the inter-visit medicament of choice and will act as a temporary obturation material. Odontopaste should ONLY be used if pulp extirpation is not possible (in case of irreversible pulpitis).

Conventional GIC (Fuji Triage (Pink), ChemFil Rock Contrast white) should be used to seal the access cavity. Don't forget to place cotton pellets over the canal entrances before placing GIC and to check the occlusion after the cement has set completely.
