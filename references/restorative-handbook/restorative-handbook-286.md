Using the information acquired so far, the diagnosis should be identified and recorded. Unless this can be achieved and there is a prospect of improvement, treatment involving the provision of new dentures should not normally be undertaken.

The diagnosis should be specific. For instance, an inadequate diagnosis would be 'unretentive upper complete denture' – the more specific diagnosis might be 'unretentive upper complete denture due to alveolar bone resorption'. Remember, a good 'rule of thumb' for all clinicians is not to initiate a treatment plan without a diagnosis firmly established – 'No Diagnosis – No Treatment'.
