## Fluoride containing products (mouthwash and tooth paste)

| Product | Regimen | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sodium fluoride mouthwash, 0.05% | Rinse mouth for 1 min with 10 ml once daily then spit | Use preferably to a different time then brushing |
| Sodium fluoride tooth paste, 0.619% (Duraphat 2800 ppm F-) | Brush teeth 2 times daily after meals for 2 min with 1 cm of paste then spit foam | Avoid rinsing, drinking or eating for 30 min after use |
| Sodium fluoride tooth paste, 1.1% (Duraphat 5000 ppm F-) | Brush teeth 3 times daily after meals for 3 min with 2 cm of paste then spit foam | Avoid rinsing, drinking or eating for 30 min |
