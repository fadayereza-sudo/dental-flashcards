## Complaint (C/O)

A summary of the patients' complaints, where possible using the patient's own words.

- Indicate what is worrying the patient
- Help establish rapport by showing empathy

Typical presenting complaints may be:

- Pain, discomfort, unhappy with appearance, bad taste, difficulty chewing, lumps, bumps, ulcers, loose dentures etc.

## History of the presenting complaint (HPC)

This is where the background to the presenting complaint(s) are written in digest form.

Try to determine:

- Where is the problem and when was it first noticed?
- Is it continuous or intermittent? If intermittent, how frequently?
- Problem becoming worse, better or about the same?
- Any previous treatments carried out to the tooth/area in question?
- Similar problem(s) in the past? Affecting quality of life?

Check with the patient if there are any more complaints, take full history for each complaint.
