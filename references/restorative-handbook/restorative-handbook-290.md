All dentures and patients are different, and the sequence of the denture stages varies. The following table shows some of the more likely treatment sequences; you should discuss with your tutor which one is most suitable for your patient.

| Stage | Conventional complete dentures | Copy / replica complete dentures | Simple cobalt chrome partial dentures and simple acrylic partial dentures | Complex partial denture cases including provision of multiple crowns or changes to OVD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary impressions | Moulds of existing dentures | Primary impressions | Primary impressions |
| 2 | Secondary impressions | Record occlusion using replicas. Design. (May take wash impression at this stage) | Primary jaw registration | Primary jaw registration |
| 3 | Record occlusion | Wax try in | Tooth modification / Secondary impressions | Wax try in to establish desired OVD / Aesthetic changes. Wash impression if not taken previously |
| 4 | Wax try in | Fit | Try in metal framework. Add wax and record occlusion* | Carry out tooth modifications / Crowns using wax try-in for clinician and technician as a guide to work to |
| 5 | Fit | Review | Wax try in | Secondary impression |
| 6 | Review | — | Fit | Try in metal framework incorporating previous wax try in |
| 7 | — | — | Review | Fit |
| 8 | — | — | — | Review |

*The metal try in should be carried out without any wax record rims on the framework. If time allows and you feel you have sufficient technical ability wax rims may be created at chairside and the occlusion recorded, otherwise the metal framework should be returned to the lab for the rims to be added.
