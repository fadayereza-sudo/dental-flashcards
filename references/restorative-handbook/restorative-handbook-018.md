## Acute Apical Periodontitis

- **C/O Pain:** Pain on biting, very tender to put teeth together. Disturbs sleep.
- **Special tests:** TTP present. Negative sensitivity test.
- **Clinically:** Deep carious lesion / crowned tooth.
- **Radiograph:** Widening of Periodontal membrane space (PMS) (e.g. LL6 distal).
- **Treatment:** RCT / Extract.
