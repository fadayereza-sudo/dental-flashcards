## Antibiotic & antifungal mouthwash (Chlorhexidine)

Chlorhexidine is a broad-spectrum biocide effective against Gram-positive and negative bacteria and fungi. Some indications: plaque inhibition, gingivitis and adjunct to management of oral candidiasis and aphthous ulcers. For Denture stomatitis, cleanse and soak dentures in mouthwash solution for 15 minutes twice daily.

**Chlorhexidine gluconate mouthwash, 0.2%**
- Rinse mouth with 10 ml for 1 min then spit 2 times daily
- One 300ml bottle
- Wait at least 30 min after brushing or rinse your mouth fully with water before using the mouthwash
- Prolonged use can lead to temporary taste disturbance and non-permanent staining of teeth and tongue
