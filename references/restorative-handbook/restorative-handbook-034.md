## Articaine 4% and adrenaline 1:100,000 - Espestesin (3M)

1 cartridge (1.7ml) contains 68 mg of articaine and 17 μg of adrenaline.
1 ml contains: 40 mg of articaine and 10 μg of adrenaline.

- **Indication:** Procedures requiring prolonged anaesthesia, do not use for ID block.
- **Dose:** Adults: Suggested maximum dose: 4 cartridges of 1.7ml in a healthy 70Kg adult. Do not exceed 0.175 ml/kg. Children (over 4 years of age): Do not exceed 7 mg/kg.
- **Length of anaesthesia:** Longer duration when compared to lidocaine, prilocaine and mepivacaine.
- **Pregnancy and lactation:** Do not use it, as no clinical experience in this situation is available.
- **Warnings and precautions:** Use with care in patients with: Lung disorders particularly severe bronchial asthma or allergic asthma; Liver or kidney or thyroid problems; Hardening of the arteries; Diabetes mellitus; Narrow-angle glaucoma; Pheochromocytoma; History of epilepsy.
- **Contraindications:** Allergy to components of the solution; Patients with: Heart problems (low/high blood pressure, irregular heartbeat, angina, heart failure or history of recent heart surgery or heart attack); Deficiency in plasma cholinesterase activity; Blood disorders including tendency to bleed or bruise easily; Taking or recently taking medicines for depression such as monoamine oxidase (MAO) inhibitors or tricyclic antidepressants.
