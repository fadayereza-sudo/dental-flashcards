## Reversible pulpitis (transient irritation of pulp)

- **C/O Pain:** Sharp triggered by stimulus. Lasts less than 30 seconds. Not spontaneous.
- **Special tests:** Not TTP. May be able to trigger with ethyl chloride.
- **Clinically:** Caries / exposed dentine.
- **Radiograph:** Primary / secondary caries, no PA changes.
- **Treatment:** Restoration / seal exposed dentine.
