# Endodontic prognosis — evidence sources

All verbatim quotes confirmed in PubMed abstract.

## 1. Ng et al. 2011 Part 1 — primary RCT UK cohort (periapical health)
- *Int Endod J* 2011;44(7):583-609.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2591.2011.01872.x | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21366626/
- Design: Prospective cohort, UCL Eastman (London, UK). 1,170 roots / 702 teeth / 534 patients, 2-4 yr follow-up.
- Verbatim: "proportion of roots with complete periapical healing after 1°RCTx (83%; 95% CI: 81%, 85%) or 2°RCTx (80%; 95% CI: 78%, 82%) were similar."
- Modifier list (abstract): preoperative absence of periapical lesion, smaller lesion size, absence of sinus tract, canal patency, instrumentation close to apical terminus, EDTA + NaOCl washes, avoiding 2% CHX adjunct, absence of extrusion, satisfactory coronal restoration.

## 2. Ng et al. 2011 Part 2 — primary RCT UK cohort (tooth survival)
- *Int Endod J* 2011;44(7):610-625.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2591.2011.01873.x | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21366627/
- Design: Prospective Cox regression, 759 primary + 858 secondary teeth, 2-4 yr follow-up.
- Verbatim: "The 4-year cumulative tooth survival following 1°RCTx [95.4% (93.6%, 96.8%)] or 2°RCTx [95.3% (93.6%, 96.5%)] was similar... The presence of pre-operative pain had a profound effect on tooth loss within the first 22 months after treatment [hazard ratio (HR) = 3.1; P = 0.001]... Patency at the apical terminus reduced tooth loss (HR = 0.3; P < 0.01) within the first 22 months... Extrusion of gutta-percha root filling... significantly increased the hazard of tooth loss beyond 22 months (HR = 3.0; P = 0.003)."

## 3. Ng et al. 2007 — primary RCT SR
- *Int Endod J* 2007;40(12):921-939.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2591.2007.01322.x | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17931389/
- Design: SR + random-effects meta-analysis, 63 studies.
- Verbatim (strict-criteria pooled range): "between 68% and 85% when strict criteria were used."

## 4. Ng et al. 2008 — retreatment SR
- *Int Endod J* 2008;41(12):1026-1046.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2591.2008.01484.x | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19133093/
- Design: SR + meta-analysis, 17 studies.
- Verbatim: "The pooled weighted success rate of 2° RCT judged by complete healing was 76.7% (95% CI 73.6%, 89.6%) and by incomplete healing, 77.2% (95% CI 61.1%, 88.1%)."

## 5. de Chevigny et al. 2008 (Toronto Phase 4) — modifiers with numbers
- *J Endod* 2008;34(3):258-263.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joen.2007.10.017 | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18291271/
- Design: Prospective cohort, 510 teeth pooled across Phases 1-4, 4-6 yr follow-up.
- Verbatim: "439 of 510 teeth (86%) were healed... radiolucency (odds ratio [OR], 2.86; confidence interval [CI], 1.56-5.24; healed: absent, 93%; present, 82%)... number of roots (OR, 2.53; CI, 1.25-5.13; healed: single, 93%; multiple, 84%)... intraoperative complications (OR, 2.27; CI, 1.05-4.89; healed: absent, 84%; present, 69%)... root-filling technique (OR, 1.89; CI, 1.01-3.53; healed: lateral, 77%; vertical, 87%)."
- Population: University of Toronto, Canada.

## 6. Bjørndal et al. 2010 — stepwise vs complete excavation
- *Eur J Oral Sci* 2010;118(3):290-297.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0722.2010.00731.x | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20572864/
- Design: Multicentre RCT (Denmark + Sweden), 314 adults, 1-year follow-up.
- Verbatim: "Stepwise excavation resulted in fewer pulp exposures compared with direct complete excavation [difference: 11.4%, 95% CI (1.2; 21.3)]... At 1 yr of follow-up, there was a statistically significantly higher success rate with stepwise excavation... [difference: 11.7%, 95% CI (0.5; 22.5)]... [direct pulp capping vs partial pulpotomy] 31.8% and 34.5%; difference: 2.7%, 95% CI (-22.7; 26.6)."

## 7. Gillen et al. 2011 — coronal restoration quality on endo outcome
- *J Endod* 2011;37(7):895-902.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joen.2011.04.002 | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21689541/
- Design: SR + meta-analysis, 9 articles.
- Verbatim: "AR/AE versus AR/IE (OR = 2.734; 95% CI, 2.61-2.88; P < .001)... AR/AE versus IR/AE (OR = 2.808; 95% CI, 2.64-2.97; P < .001)... there is no significant difference in the odds of healing between" AR/IE and IR/AE.

## 8. Cushley et al. 2019 — full pulpotomy in irreversible pulpitis
- *J Dent* 2019;88:103158.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jdent.2019.06.005 | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31229496/
- Design: SR of 8 articles, UK-authored.
- Verbatim: "The average success rate for coronal pulpotomy was 97.4% clinical and 95.4% radiographic at 12 month follow-up. This was reduced to 93.97% clinical and 88.39% radiographic success at 36 months follow-up... evidence... is based on heterogeneous studies with high risk of bias... well-designed, adequately powered randomised controlled trials [are called for]."
