# Revised prognosis framework

**Citation:** Kwok V, Caton JG. Commentary: Prognosis revisited: a system for assigning periodontal prognosis. *J Periodontol* 2007;78(11):2063-2071.

## Framework categories

Kwok and Caton re-anchor prognosis to the stability of the supporting periodontium given what can realistically be treated and maintained, rather than to a guess about long-term tooth survival.

- **Favorable:** comprehensive treatment and patient compliance will stabilise the periodontium.
- **Questionable:** local or systemic factors may or may not be controllable; stability cannot be guaranteed.
- **Unfavorable:** factors cannot be controlled; periodontal breakdown is likely.
- **Hopeless:** tooth must be extracted.

## Summary of the argument

"Prognosis can be based on stability of the periodontal supporting apparatus, which is influenced by more evidence-based factors. Systems based on tooth loss were unpredictable over the long term."

General factors considered: smoking, diabetes, compliance, systemic disease. Local factors: furcation, cemento-enamel projections, tooth position, overhangs, trauma-from-occlusion, mobility.

## Take-home

Compatible with the 2017 BSP/EFP staging and grading taxonomy. Think "will this tooth's periodontium stay stable with what we can realistically deliver?" rather than "will this tooth survive ten years?".
