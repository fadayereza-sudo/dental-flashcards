# Indirect restorations — evidence sources

All verbatim quotes confirmed in PubMed abstract unless explicitly flagged.

## 1. Pjetursson et al. 2007 — single crowns (Part I SR)
- Pjetursson BE, Sailer I, Zwahlen M, Hämmerle CHF. *Clin Oral Implants Res* 2007;18 Suppl 3:73-85.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0501.2007.01467.x | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17594372/
- Design: Systematic review + meta-analysis (34 studies).
- Verbatim: "the 5-year survival of all-ceramic crowns was estimated at 93.3% (95% CI: 91.1-95%) and 95.6% (95% CI: 92.4-97.5%) for metal-ceramic crowns. Densely sintered alumina (Procera technique) crowns showed the highest 5-year survival rate of 96.4%... A significantly lower survival rate of 87.5% was calculated for glass-ceramic crowns after 5 years... lower survival rates of 90.4% and 84.4% can be expected for InCeram crowns and glass-ceramic crowns when utilized for premolars and molars."
- Population: Predominantly European university-clinic cohorts.

## 2. Sailer et al. 2015 — updated all-ceramic SR
- Sailer I, Makarov NA, Thoma DS, Zwahlen M, Pjetursson BE. *Dent Mater* 2015;31(6):603-623.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dental.2015.02.011 | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25842099/
- Design: Meta-analysis (67 studies, 14,097 crowns).
- Verbatim: "estimated survival rate of metal-ceramic SCs of 94.7% (95% CI: 94.1-96.9%)... leucit or lithium-disilicate reinforced glass ceramic SCs (96.6%; 95% CI: 94.9-96.7%), of glass infiltrated alumina SCs (94.6%; 95% CI: 92.7-96%) and densely sintered alumina and zirconia SCs (96%; 95% CI: 93.8-97.5%; 92.1%; 95% CI: 82.8-95.6%)... Densely sintered zirconia SCs were more frequently lost due to veneering ceramic fractures than metal-ceramic SCs... Zirconia-based SCs should not be considered as primary option due to their high incidence of technical problems."
- Population: Mostly European.

## 3. Tan et al. 2004 — conventional FPD SR
- Tan K, Pjetursson BE, Lang NP, Chan ESY. *Clin Oral Implants Res* 2004;15(6):654-666.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0501.2004.01119.x | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15533126/
- Design: Meta-analysis, 19 studies.
- Verbatim: "The 10-year probability of survival for fixed partial dentures was 89.1% (95% CI: 81-93.8%) while the probability of success was 71.1% (95% CI: 47.7-85.2%). The 10-year risk for caries and periodontitis leading to FPD loss was 2.6% and 0.7%, respectively. The 10-year risk for loss of retention was 6.4%, for abutment fracture 2.1% and for material fractures 3.2%."

## 4. Pjetursson et al. 2004 — cantilever FPDs
- Pjetursson BE, Tan K, Lang NP, Brägger U, Egger M, Zwahlen M. *Clin Oral Implants Res* 2004;15(6):667-676.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0501.2004.01120.x | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15533127/
- Verbatim: "estimated survival rate of cantilever FPDs of 81.8% (95% CI: 78.2-84.9%) and success rate (free of all complications) of 63% (95% CI: 54.7-70.2%) after 10 years... survival and success rates of cantilever fixed partial dentures were lower than those of conventional end-abutment supported FPDs."

## 5. Thoma et al. 2017 — resin-bonded bridges
- Thoma DS, Sailer I, Ioannidis A, Zwahlen M, Makarov N, Pjetursson BE. *Clin Oral Implants Res* 2017;28(11):1421-1432.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/clr.13007 | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28191679/
- Design: Meta-analysis, 29 studies, 2,300 RBBs.
- Verbatim: "estimated survival of resin-bonded bridges of 91.4% (95% CI: 86.7-94.4%) after 5 years and 82.9% (95% CI: 73.2-89.3%) after 10 years. RBBs with one retainer had a significantly higher survival rate (P < 0.0001) and a lower de-bonding rate (P = 0.001) compared with RBBs retained by two or more retainers... The most frequent complications were de-bonding (loss of retention), which occurred in 15% (95% CI: 10.9-20.6%)."

## 6. Figueiredo et al. 2015 — posts and root fracture
- Figueiredo FED, Martins-Filho PRS, Faria-E-Silva AL. *J Endod* 2015;41(3):309-316.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joen.2014.10.006 | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25459568/
- Design: Meta-analysis, 7 RCTs + 7 cohorts.
- Verbatim: "The pooled survival rate was 90% (95% CI 85.5-93.3) for metal-based posts and 83.9% (95% CI 67.6-92.8) for fiber-reinforced posts. The overall incidence rate of root fractures (catastrophic failures) was similar between metal and fiber posts. Prefabricated metal posts and carbon fiber posts had a 2-fold increase in the incidence rate of root fractures compared with cast metal posts and glass fiber posts, respectively."

## 7. Beier et al. 2012 — veneer 20-year cohort
- Beier US, Kapferer I, Burtscher D, Dumfahrt H. *Int J Prosthodont* 2012;25(1):79-85.
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22259802/ (no indexed DOI)
- Design: Retrospective single-centre cohort, 84 patients / 318 veneers, 1987-2009.
- Verbatim: "The estimated survival rate was 94.4% after 5 years, 93.5% at 10 years, and 82.93% at 20 years. Nonvital teeth showed a significantly higher failure risk (P = .0012). There was a 7.7-times greater risk of failure associated with existing parafunction (bruxism, P = .0004). Marginal discoloration was significantly greater in smokers (P ≤ .01). The main reason for failure was fracture of the ceramic (44.83%)."
- Population: Austria (Innsbruck); single-centre European.

## 8. Goodacre et al. 2003 — complication rate hierarchy
- Goodacre CJ, Bernal G, Rungcharassaeng K, Kan JYK. *J Prosthet Dent* 2003;90(1):31-41.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/s0022-3913(03)00214-2 | PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12869972/
- Design: Structured review of 50 years of English-language clinical data.
- Verbatim: "The lowest incidence of clinical complications was associated with all-ceramic crowns (8%). Posts and cores (10%) and conventional single crowns (11%) had comparable clinical complications incidences. Resin-bonded prostheses (26%) and conventional fixed partial dentures (27%) were found to have comparable clinical complications incidences."
