# Effect of SPT compliance on tooth loss

**Primary citation:** Costa FO, Lages EJP, Cota LOM, Lorentz TCM, Soares RV, Cortelli JR. Tooth loss in individuals under periodontal maintenance therapy: 5-year prospective study. *J Periodontal Res* 2014;49(1):121-128.

**Supporting meta-analysis:** Lee CT, Huang HY, Sun TC, Karimbux N. Impact of Patient Compliance on Tooth Loss during Supportive Periodontal Therapy: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. *J Dent Res* 2015;94(6):777-786.

## Verbatim

"TL was significantly lower among RC (0.12 teeth lost/year) in comparison to IC (0.36 teeth lost/year; p < 0.01)." (Costa 2014)

"The risk of tooth loss in the RC group was significantly lower than that in the EC group (pooled RRTL: 0.56 [CI: 0.38, 0.82])." (Lee 2015)

## Headline numbers

- Regular compliers: 0.12 teeth lost per year.
- Irregular compliers: 0.36 teeth lost per year (3x the rate, p < 0.01).
- Pooled risk ratio for tooth loss, regular vs erratic: 0.56 (95% CI 0.38-0.82).
- Weighted mean difference in tooth loss rate: -0.12 teeth (CI -0.19 to -0.05).

## Modifiers in Costa

- Age >55.
- Male sex.
- Smoking (smoker IC loses more teeth than smoker RC).

## Population caveats

Costa is a Brazilian specialist-clinic cohort; Lee pools studies including Axelsson (Swedish) and Costa (Brazilian). Both settings are specialist practices, not NHS primary care.

## Take-home

A patient who won't commit to recall is not a great candidate for elaborate restorative work on questionable teeth. Missing recalls roughly triples tooth-loss rate and should be documented as a meaningful change in the patient's risk profile.
