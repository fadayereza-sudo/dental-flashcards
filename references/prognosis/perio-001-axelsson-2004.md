# Long-term tooth retention under SPT

**Citation:** Axelsson P, Nyström B, Lindhe J. The long-term effect of a plaque control program on tooth mortality, caries and periodontal disease in adults. Results after 30 years of maintenance. *J Clin Periodontol* 2004;31(9):749-757.

## Verbatim

"Few teeth were lost during the 30 years of maintenance; 0.4 to 1.8 in different age cohorts."

"The incidence of caries and periodontal disease as well as tooth mortality in this subject sample was very small."

## Headline numbers

- 0.4 to 1.8 teeth lost per subject over 30 years (varies by baseline age cohort: 20-35, 36-50, 51-70).
- Of teeth lost, only 21 across the entire cohort were attributable to caries or periodontitis; the primary cause was root fracture.

## Modifiers in the protocol

- Recall interval individualised to risk (range 2 to 36 months).
- Protocol included professional mechanical plaque removal and fluoride application, not purely SPT.
- Delivered by hygienists in a specialist recall programme.

## Population caveats for a UK GDP

Swedish, ethnically homogeneous, specialist-practice cohort. The care-delivery model is not an NHS 15-minute check-up. Treat the figures as a ceiling of what is achievable under individualised risk-based recall, not as an average NHS outcome.
