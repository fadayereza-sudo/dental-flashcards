# Teeth vs implants in periodontally compromised patients

**Citation:** Roccuzzo M, Imber J-C, Salvi GE, Roccuzzo A. Clinical outcomes of dental implants in patients with and without history of periodontitis: A 20-year prospective study. *J Clin Periodontol* 2022;49(12):1346-1356.

## Verbatim from abstract

"Overall implant survival rate of 93% (i.e., 94.9% for PHP, 91.8% for mPCP and 93.1% for sPCP)."

"PCP not compliant with SPC experienced implant loss with odds ratio of 14.59 (1.30-164.29, p = .03)."

## Verbatim from results (PMC full text)

"The mean number of teeth lost per patient, regardless of the reason for extraction, was 0.6 ± 1.0 for PHP, 1.3 ± 1.3 for moderate PCP and 1.9 ± 1.9 for severe PCP."

"In the PHP group, around 19.1% of subjects received adjunctive antibiotic therapy and 4.8% received surgery, while in the mPCP group, 31.0% of subjects received antibiotic therapy and 17.2% underwent surgery. Finally, in the sPCP group, 8 subjects (25.81%) received adjunctive antibiotic therapy and 10 subjects underwent surgery (32.26%)."

## Headline numbers (all abstract- or results-verbatim)

- 20-year tooth loss per patient: 0.6 (PHP), 1.3 (mPCP), 1.9 (sPCP).
- 20-year implant survival: 94.9% PHP, 91.8% mPCP, 93.1% sPCP.
- Odds ratio for implant loss in non-compliant PCP: 14.59 (95% CI 1.30 to 164.29).
- Adjunctive antibiotic / surgical intervention in sPCP: 25.8% / 32.3%.

## Population caveats

Italian specialist-practice cohort; European single-centre. Generalisable to well-maintained private patients; less so to mixed NHS cohorts with higher drop-out from recall.

## Take-home

Strategic extraction to replace a treated tooth with a "cleaner" implant is not supported by the 20-year data. Treated teeth last well over a decade; implants in the same patient fail more often and need frequent intervention. Compliance with supportive care dominates both.
