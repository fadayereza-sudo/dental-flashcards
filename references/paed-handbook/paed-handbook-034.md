Fluoride varnish:

- Fluoride varnish is one of the best options for increasing the availability of topical fluoride, regardless of the levels of fluoride in the water supply.
- Dental nurses can be trained (in accordance to Primary Care Commissioning guidance) to apply fluoride varnish to the prescription of dentists. This use of skill mix can assist a practice to become more preventively orientated.
- Duraphat (2.2% sodium fluoride, 22,600ppmF) is one of the readily available varnishes in the UK. This has a very high fluoride concentration and is supplied in a small tube.
- Great care should be taken to use it sparingly on young children to avoid toxicity.
- The manufacturer's recommended dose for children:
  - 2-5 years old (primary dentition): 0.25ml
  - >6 years old (mixed dentition): 0.40ml
  - Diagram of dispensed blob diameters: 11mm at 0.25ml, 13mm at 0.40ml.
- While a thorough prophylaxis is not essential prior to application, removal of gross plaque is advised. Teeth should then be dried with cotton wool rolls or a triple syringe. The varnish should be carefully applied with a microbrush to pits, fissures and approximal surfaces of primary and permanent teeth.
- The patient should be advised to avoid eating, drinking and rinsing out for 30 minutes and eat only soft food in the following 4 hours.
- Brushing can recommence on the day following application.
- The use of Duraphat (which contains colophony) is contraindicated in children with a history of allergic episodes requiring hospitalisation due to severe asthma or allergy, or who are allergic to sticking plaster. It is also contraindicated in patients with ulcerative gingivitis and stomatitis. In these cases, alternative fluoride varnishes without colophony such as Clinpro and Fluor Protector or other age-appropriate fluoride preparations (e.g. mouthrinse or high fluoride toothpaste) should be used or considered.
- Fluoride varnish can be applied twice per year to children 3 years and above. For all children at increased risk of caries, it can be applied two or more times per year, up to a maximum of 4 times per year.
