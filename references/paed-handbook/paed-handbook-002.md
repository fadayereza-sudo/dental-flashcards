Commonly used local anaesthetics:

- Lignocaine 2% with 1:80,000 epinephrine
- Articaine 4% with 1:100,000 epinephrine
- Prilocaine 3% with felypressin

Lignocaine 2% with epinephrine remains the most commonly used anaesthetic solution in dentistry. However in the last few years the use of articaine has increased. There is some limited evidence that in young children infiltration with 4% articaine gives as profound analgesia as IDB with lignocaine for restoration of mandibular posterior teeth, including for pulp therapy in primary molars. ARTICAINE SHOULD NEVER BE USED FOR AN IDB.
