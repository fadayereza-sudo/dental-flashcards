- Placing resin sealant can be difficult on a partially erupted tooth, or with a child whose co-operation is limited and/or moisture control is poor.
- High viscosity, self-cured GIC can be used as a sealant material. However, the retention rates of this material are poor over the long term, so it should be used as a temporary measure only. GIC sealants achieve successful caries prevention by releasing fluoride even when partially lost.
- The 'press finger' technique can be deployed for a child who is not able to tolerate the resin sealant procedure.
