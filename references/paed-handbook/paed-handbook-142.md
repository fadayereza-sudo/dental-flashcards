**Other Forms of Sedation**

- **Nasal / trans-mucosal:**
  - Usually use midazolam.
  - Not generally used in this department.
- **Rectal:**
  - Usually use diazepam.
  - Not generally used in this department.
- **Intravenous:**
  - Not recommended for patients under 16 years old.
  - Occasionally used on phobic adolescents [12–15 years old].
  - Use a titrated dose of midazolam as the standard intravenous sedation technique.
  - Should not be given in Primary Care.
