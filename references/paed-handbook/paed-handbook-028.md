Diet analysis involves a more thorough analysis of the diet of children with increased risk of caries using the 3-day diet diary record.

- The child or parent/carer keeps a record of all food and drink consumed over a 3-day period, with at least one of the days being at the weekend. The diary is collected on the subsequent visit. Analysis is made of the diary and advice offered as necessary on the following visit.
- During analysis of the diet diary, it is essential to assess the overall pattern of eating to establish the following information:
  - The number of intakes of food and drinks per day
  - The number of intakes that contain added sugars and how many were consumed between normal mealtimes
  - Whether any intakes containing sugars were taken within one hour of bedtime (when the caries protective effects of saliva are reduced)
- The use of a diet diary is a good way of helping parents/carers to identify the frequency and amount of sugary intake.
- It is also a good opportunity for the dental team to provide dietary advice for general health.
