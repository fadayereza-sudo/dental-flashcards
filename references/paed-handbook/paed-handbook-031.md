Fluoride toothpastes:

- Fluoride is added to toothpaste in one of the following forms:
  - Sodium fluoride
  - Sodium monofluorophosphate (MFP)
  - Stannous/amine fluoride
- Parents/carers should be advised and shown to read the labels of over-the-counter toothpastes to look for the parts per million of fluoride (ppmF) in the toothpaste.
- National guideline regarding the correct amount and appropriate concentration of over-the-counter toothpaste has been mentioned in the toothbrushing section earlier and should be followed.
- For older children and young adults with high caries risk (those with active caries, orthodontic appliances, highly cariogenic diet and on medication), high concentration fluoride toothpaste can be prescribed:
  - 10+ years old: 2,800ppm sodium fluoride (0.619%DPF)
  - 16+ years old: 5,000ppm sodium fluoride (1.1% DPF)
- For people who are sensitive to strong flavours in toothpaste or with special needs, OraNurse can be recommended. This is an unflavoured and non-foaming toothpaste with 1,450ppm sodium fluoride suitable for 4+ years old.
