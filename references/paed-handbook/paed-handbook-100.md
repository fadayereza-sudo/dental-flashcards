Only a practitioner who is willing to discipline him/herself to the routine use of rubber dam for a given time will be able to recognise the full extent of its value as a method aiding superior results. The finest possible restorative care is thereby achieved.

**Advantages of rubber dam**

Rubber dam makes dental care for children easier and a higher standard of dental care can be achieved in less time than would otherwise be required. It aids the child to develop a sense of security as the "raincoat" isolates him/her from the drill, air, water and assortment of dental materials placed in the mouth. In addition, it retracts the cheeks and tongue, and assists in moisture control thus provides better access and visibility to the dentist.

The benefits of rubber dam placement can be categorised into 3 main categories:

1. **Safety:** Rubber dam prevents damages of soft tissues by rotary and hand instruments as well as medicaments used in the provision of endodontic and other care. In addition, it greatly reduces the risk of foreign body swallowing or inhalation. Also, the risk of cross-infection to dentist and staff caused by the distribution of an aerosol of the patient's saliva by the high-speed rotary instruments around the operating room is largely reduced by proper placement of rubber dam.

2. **Benefits to the child:** Rubber dam isolates the operative area from the vital functions of the patient (sight, hearing, breathing and swallowing) thus reduces the child's anxiety and helps him/her to relax during the dental treatment.

3. **Benefits to the dentist:** Rubber dam reduces the dentist stress by reducing the effort required by the operator to protect the soft tissues of the mouth and airways. Moreover, it retracts the tongue and cheeks. Correctly placed rubber dam will gently pull the cheeks and tongue away from the operative area, allowing the operator a better view of the area treated. In addition, it retracts the gingival tissues gently away from the cervical margin of the tooth, making it easier to see the extent of any caries close to the margin and often bringing the cervical margin of a prepared cavity above the level of the gingival margin, thus making restoration considerably easier.

Finally, rubber dam aids in moisture control. It is the only technique that readily ensures a dry field.
