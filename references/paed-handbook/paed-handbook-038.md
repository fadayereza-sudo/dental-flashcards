- Since the development of sealants there has been a question regarding the effect of sealing over caries, the concern being that caries will progress unidentified under the sealant. A number of trials have examined this by actively sealing over caries, all have shown that sealants arrest or slow the rate of caries progression.
- 'If in doubt, seal'. However, this should be followed by regular clinical and radiographic monitoring until caries status is confirmed.
- One instance where actively sealing over caries is to be recommended is in the pre-cooperative child where the placement of sealant may help acclimatise the child, with the added benefit of controlling the caries, until a definitive restoration can be placed. This can also be achieved with a well-placed temporary restoration to seal a cavitated lesion.
