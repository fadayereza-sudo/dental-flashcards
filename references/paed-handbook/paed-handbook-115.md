**Localised Anterior Cross-bite**

Early correction of an anterior cross-bite encourages the development of a class I occlusion provided there is a normal skeletal pattern, adequate space in the arch and an adequate overbite to self-retain the proclined tooth after correction. It also avoids damage to the labial surface of the tooth in cross-bite.

The essential features of a removable appliance for correction of an anterior cross-bite are:

- Active component e.g. Z-spring, palatal screw, T-spring.
- Retention as far anteriorly as possible to resist the tendency of the spring to displace the front of the appliance.
- Posterior capping to open the occlusion while the upper incisor moves labially.

Correction of an anterior cross-bite will take approximately three months. Once the cross-bite is corrected the posterior capping should be removed from the appliance and the patient directed to continue to wear the appliance for 6 weeks full-time following which the appliance wear can be discontinued.
