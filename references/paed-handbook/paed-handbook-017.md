It is important, when speaking to children, especially the very young, to use words and terms to which they can relate. Paediatric dentists often call this "childrenese." The key features of effective "childrenese" are that the terms used for the various pieces of dental equipment and procedures are constructed from words which are probably already in the child's vocabulary and which carry little or no negative connotations.

"Childrenese" terms for dental equipment can include:

| Childrenese term | Dental item/procedure |
|---|---|
| buzzy bee | slow handpiece/electric toothbrush |
| whizzy brush | air rotor |
| magic wind | triplespray/inhalation sedation |
| jungle juice or sleepy juice | local anaesthetic |
| spray your teeth off to sleep | giving a local anaesthetic |
| rubber raincoat/umbrella | rubber dam |
| clip or button/tooth ring | rubber dam clamp |
| Hoover | suction |

(Based on Fayle & Crawford, 1997)
