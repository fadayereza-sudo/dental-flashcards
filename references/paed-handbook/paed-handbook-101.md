**The Procedure**

- Children's Rubber Dam Kits are provided on clinic. These should be set up with holes punched and correct clamp selected and ready prior to the child coming into the surgery.
- Students should have help from a nurse or colleague when applying the dam.
- As with all dental procedures eye protection for staff and patients is essential.
- The gingivae (including palatal/lingual) MUST be anaesthetised before applying a clamp.
- WINGLESS clamps are used and selected as follows:
  - DW: primary molars / premolars
  - BW: permanent molars
  - FW: partially erupted molars
- Floss MUST be tied to the clamp before application.
- The rubber dam should be pre-punched. Three overlapping holes are adequate for single tooth isolation. 5–6 holes, overlapping in a line are required for isolation of a quadrant (trough technique).
- Clamp is applied first (WITHOUT the rubber dam).

Note the grip on the forceps; it is held palm upwards and orientated so that it is pointing inwards (i.e. towards the operator).

When the clamp is secure, the rubber dam is applied. The frame is then attached to support the dam.

In quadrant isolation, the trough is stretched to include all the required teeth and can be secured with a widget or wizard wedge.

Be aware that saliva/moisture can accumulate beneath the dam in the mouth which may be uncomfortable for the patient. This should be removed regularly with a saliva ejector; many children can hold the saliva ejector effectively. At the end of the procedure the dam is removed by simply engaging the clamp with the forceps and removing everything (clamp, dam and frame) in one go.
