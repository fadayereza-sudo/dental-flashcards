**Anomalies of Root Form**

**Taurodontism:**

- This applies to multi-rooted teeth where the body is enlarged corono-apically at the expense of the roots.
- Uncommon in the primary dentition.
- Prevalence of about 6.3%.

**Accessory roots:**

- These may occur in almost any tooth.
- More commonly seen in lower canines, premolars and molars.
- Prevalence in the primary dentition 1–9%, in the permanent dentition figures range from 1–45%.
- Teeth with accessory roots have larger crowns than normal and possible association with supernumerary teeth.
