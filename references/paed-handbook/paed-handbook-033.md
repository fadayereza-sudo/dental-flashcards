(Casein phosphopeptide-amorphous calcium phosphate crème- CPP-ACP/Tooth Mousse Plus (CPP-ACPF) and known as MI paste and MIPlus in the USA)

- CPP-ACP and CPP-ACPF are available as crème (Tooth Mousse and Tooth Mousse Plus) for topical application at home.
- CPP-ACPF releases fluoride, calcium and phosphate ions for local remineralisation of enamel.
- These are to be applied to surfaces at risk of caries, erosion or with white spot lesions.
- The crème is applied to teeth after brushing by smearing across tooth surfaces with a clean finger or cotton-bud. The crème should not be rinsed out.
- Tooth Mousse Plus contains 900ppmF and should not be used by children 6 years and under.
- The crème is also contraindicated in people with milk protein (casein) allergy.
