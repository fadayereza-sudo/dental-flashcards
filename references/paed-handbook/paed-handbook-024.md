Timing of brushing:

- Brush as soon as the first primary tooth erupts
  - A damp cotton flannel or face cloth on a finger can be used to remove the plaque in infants.
- Brush twice daily as a minimum (last thing at night before bed and at one other time each day)
  - Some parents/carers find it difficult to get the child to brush in the morning especially during school term time. Stress the importance of night brushing and find time to brush one other time when more co-operation can be obtained from the child.
  - A simple message for patients is 'brush your teeth first thing in the morning and last thing at night'.

Fluoride toothpaste:

- Use the correct amount of toothpaste with age appropriate fluoride concentration
  - 0-3 years old: small smear of toothpaste, >1,000ppm fluoride
  - 3-6 years old: pea-sized toothpaste, >1,000ppm fluoride
  - 0-6 years old with high caries risk: small smear/pea-sized, 1,350-1,500ppm fluoride
  - >7 years old: 1,350-1,500ppm fluoride
