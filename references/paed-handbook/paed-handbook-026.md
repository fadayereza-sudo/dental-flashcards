Plaque disclosing agents:

- Use plaque disclosing agents to show the patient plaque deposits on their teeth.
- Let the patient brush their teeth and attempt to remove all the staining in order to learn the best brushing technique. Ask for parent/carer's help if needed depending on child's age.
- Record plaque free score (PFS) in notes and try to set an achievable target as close to 100% as possible.
- Advise on the use of disclosing tablets at home as a learning tool. For the first week, advise patient to disclose first, then brush. After one week, brush first, and then disclose in order to identify areas that are being missed.

Toothbrushing charts:

- Child or parent/carer can use this to record each time the child's teeth are brushed as a reminder about brushing frequency.
- This can also serve as a reward chart to motivate the child to brush.

Toothbrushing timer:

- A timer might help motivate the child to ensure thorough brushing.
- There are lots of applications available on smart technology nowadays to make brushing fun and help children learn through play.
