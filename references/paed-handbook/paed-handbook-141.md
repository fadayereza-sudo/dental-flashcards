**Oral Sedation**

**Indications**

- For use when inhalation and intravenous sedation are not suitable e.g. patients with special care requirements or extreme needle-phobia.
- Can be used in combination with other sedation techniques.
- Ideal for very young children for short procedure e.g. extraction of a traumatised upper primary incisor.

**Procedure**

- Benzodiazepines usually midazolam 0.3–0.5 mg/kg [maximum dose 20 mg].
- Can have an unpredictable effect.
- Inability to alter sedation level.
- Sedative & amnesic.
- Time to onset of sedation approx 40 mins.
- Duration of effect approx 30 mins.
- Recovery time approx 30 mins.
- Can be given with paracetamol to address pain management.
