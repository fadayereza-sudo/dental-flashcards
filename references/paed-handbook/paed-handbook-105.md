**Stages of Pulpotomy**

1. Administer local analgesia and apply rubber dam wherever possible.
2. Remove caries and identify the site of pulp exposure. If there is no pulp exposure then access to the pulp chamber is made from the base of the cavity.
3. Remove roof of the pulp chamber. When the bur passes through the roof of the chamber a "dip" is felt. Once this is felt the bur is not taken any deeper but moved sideways to remove the roof of the pulp chamber.
4. Remove coronal pulp with a large excavator.
5. Apply ferric sulphate on a cotton pledget, to the radicular pulp.
6. Remove the cotton pledget and check that there is no excessive haemorrhage from the remaining pulpal tissue.
7. Fill chamber with zinc oxide eugenol cement, pressing on the zinc oxide with a damp pledget to make sure that it is well condensed in the pulp chamber.
8. Place coronal restoration, preferably a stainless steel crown.
