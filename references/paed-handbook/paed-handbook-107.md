**Stages of the Hall Crown Technique**

1. Place orthodontic separators on the child first visit to the dental clinic to create interproximal space and help placement of the crown at the child next appointment.
2. Place gauze inside the patient's mouth to protect their airway.
3. Try different crown sizes over the tooth until the correct size is found; the crown should cover the cusps of the tooth and give the feeling of spring back when pushed as far as, but not beyond, the contact point.
4. The crown is then lined with glass ionomer cement and seated onto the tooth using finger pressure or by asking the child to bite on a cotton roll.
5. Remove excess glass ionomer cement and floss between contact points.
