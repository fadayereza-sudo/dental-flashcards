**Definition**

Dental anomalies are associated with defects in tooth development and may be caused by hereditary, systemic, traumatic or local factors (e.g. drug). This section will briefly describe the more common anomalies that you may come across.

**Tooth Number**

- Alteration in tooth number occurs usually during initiation of the dental lamina stage of dental development.
- The alteration may produce extra or missing teeth.

**Extra teeth: Supernumeraries**

Supernumerary teeth are common:

- 0.2–0.8% primary dentition (Caucasian)
- 1.5–3.5% permanent dentition (Male to female ratio of about 2:1)

They may be described according to their form and their position:

- **Form:**
  - Conical (peg shaped)
  - Tuberculate (barrel shaped)
  - Supplemental
  - Odontome
- **Position:**
  - Mesiodens (in the midline)
  - Distomolar (distal to the arch)
  - Paramolar (lingual or buccal to the arch)
  - The most common position for a permanent supernumerary is the premaxilla.
