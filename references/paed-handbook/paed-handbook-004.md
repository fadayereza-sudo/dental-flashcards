**Wand Local Anaesthetic System**

The Wand is a computerised local anaesthetic system that is able to deliver local anaesthetic solution at a fixed speed. The system is made up of microprocessor control unit, disposable hand piece and foot control (activation switch).

The Wand delivers local anaesthetic at a slow and fixed rate which is thought to give rise to 'pain free local anaesthesia'. This technique can be useful in providing anaesthesia in children who are very anxious or 'needle phobic'.
