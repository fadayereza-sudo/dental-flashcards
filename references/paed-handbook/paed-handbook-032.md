Fluoride mouthrinses:

- Two types of rinses are available:
  - Daily: 0.05% w/w neutral sodium fluoride (225ppmF)
  - Weekly: 0.2% w/w neutral sodium fluoride (900ppmF)
- For those 8+ years old with high caries risk, prescribe daily fluoride rinse.
- The daily rinse is recommended as it is simpler to rinse on a regular basis than trying to remember to use a product just once a week. Moreover, maintaining a low level of fluoride in the mouth on a daily basis fits in with our understanding of the mode of action of fluoride on the remineralisation of enamel.
- Use the rinse at a different time to brushing with a fluoride toothpaste so that the number of fluoride exposures increases. The effect of toothbrushing and rinsing with fluoride has been shown to be additive.
- All orthodontic patients should be using a daily fluoride rinse to minimise the risk of demineralisation and white spot lesions.
- Other groups of patients who will benefit from the prescription of a daily fluoride rinse are:
  - Patients with hyposalivation due to medication or those with congenital absence of the major salivary glands
  - Children with medical problems for whom caries could be a serious problem, eg cardiac patients, those with bleeding disorders.
- Children under 8 years of age (before eruption of the permanent incisors) should not be recommended to use fluoride mouth rinse due to the increased risk of swallowing the product and this may cause fluorosis.
