**All Tissues**

- **Arrested development of tooth germs**
  - May be a sequela to irradiation, trauma, chemotherapy.
  - Rarely it may be as a result of infection to the primary predecessor.
- **Odontodysplasia**
  - Rare and affects the sexes equally and affects the primary and permanent dentitions.
  - Also known as Ghost teeth, regional odontodysplasia.
  - Crowns of affected teeth are reduced in size and are rough with a brown stained surface, soft to the probe.
  - Teeth are affected in a regional way but may cross the midline. Maxilla is twice as commonly affected as the mandible.
