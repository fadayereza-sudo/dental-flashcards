**Equipment**

1. **Burs:** SSW 330 pear shaped bur is the standard bur for all cavity preps in posterior teeth.
2. **Handpieces:** generally small or miniature handpieces are preferred. Air turbine is to be used for the initial cavity preparation and then the slow speed with a round bur of the appropriate size to remove the caries.
3. **Composite:** as better composites become available for posterior teeth, it will be the aim to eventually cease using amalgam. Students are encouraged to use composites for small posterior occlusal or class II restorations.
4. **Resin-modified GIC:** can be used for teeth that are close to exfoliation.
5. **Matrix bands:** matrix bands must be well fitted. The preferred method is to use spot-welded bands customised for each tooth; alternatively Toffelmire or Siqveland bands may be used.
6. **Wedges:** wedges are mandatory for packing of interproximal restorations.

**Cavity Preparation and Restorations**

Minimal cavity preparation and composite restorations are preferred for the restoration of primary teeth. The remaining susceptible surfaces should be fissure sealed.
