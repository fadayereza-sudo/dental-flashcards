**GA pre-op preparation and planning**

- Ensure prevention advice has been given.
- Consider which general anaesthetic list the patient should be placed on:
  - Exodontia or comprehensive dental care
  - One-day unit or Clarendon wing (medically compromised)
- Obtain valid consent. Treatment plan and consent form must be signed by a specialist in paediatric dentistry. Give patient printed information booklet on general anaesthetics.
- Check and update patient's medical history. Highlight any specific medical concerns and liaise with other medical specialties where appropriate. Ask all patients (irrespective of racial background) if they, or anyone in their family, has sickle cell disease.
  - If there is a family history of Sickle Cell Disease (i.e. Homozygous Sickle Cell Anaemia), then results of any previous tests (eg heel prick) should be checked, or electrophoresis should be carried out to determine the patient's HbS status.
  - Patients with no family history of Sickle Cell Disease do not need to be tested prior to GA, irrespective of their racial background. Patients with a family history of trait only do not need to be tested. If the patient/guardian does not know if there is a family history of Sickle Cell Anaemia, then we should record their answer as such, but such children do not need to be tested as it is considered safe to assume that they are negative.
  - Pre-GA admission planning for patients with known homozygous Sickle Cell Anaemia will require careful liaison with the patient's haematologist and the anaesthetist.
- Document child's weight and height.
- Check and update patient's contact details including telephone number.
- Ensure radiographs (where appropriate) are available and up to date.
- Highlight any special considerations or specific treatment needs.
- Send correspondence to GDP where appropriate.
- Document follow up plan.

**Outpatient in One Day Unit**

- Dental extractions (exodontia) or comprehensive dental care (extractions, restorations).
- Children 3 years old & over, up to 16.
- Body mass index less than 30.
- No relevant medical history (ASA I & II).
- All carious teeth should be removed at time of GA to ensure patient wakes up dentally fit (also to avoid / reduce the no. of repeat GA's).
- Treatment plan must be signed by a specialist in paediatric dentistry.

**Day Case/Inpatient on Clarendon Wing**

- Comprehensive care (restorations, extractions, surgicals, ultrasonic scaling).
- Any age up to 16 years.
- Any medical history.
- All carious teeth should be treated (i.e. patient must wake up dentally fit).
- Intra-oral radiographs can be taken in theatre if unable to cope pre-op.
- Treatment plan must be signed by a specialist in paediatric dentistry.
