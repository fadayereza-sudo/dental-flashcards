## The recommendation

Once the patient is as dentally fit as feasible, carry out most routine dental treatment as normal and continue to provide personalised preventive advice. Prioritise care that reduces mucosal trauma or helps avoid future extractions or any oral surgery that impacts on bone.

## Why

The MRONJ risk attaches to procedures that breach mucosa over bone — extractions, surgical periodontal treatment, implant placement. Restorative dentistry, scaling, prosthodontics, fluoride application, and supragingival prevention do not carry the same risk and should be delivered as for any other patient. Preventive intensification (high-fluoride toothpaste, dietary advice, oral hygiene support, smoking cessation) is the cheapest way to reduce the future need for the procedures that do carry risk.

## How this applies in practice

- Higher caries-risk patients on anti-resorptives may benefit from prescription 2,800 or 5,000 ppm fluoride toothpaste.
- Refit any denture causing mucosal ulceration immediately — unhealed ulcers over bone are a documented trigger.
- Aggressive prevention of perio disease matters: untreated periodontitis is itself a risk factor for MRONJ, and progression toward extraction is exactly what you are trying to avoid.
- Routine recall and radiological review continue as normal — the patient is not "off-limits" for dentistry.
