## The recommendation

For dental abscess in penicillin-allergic patients, prescribe metronidazole 400 mg three times daily for 5 days; it is also added to amoxicillin when there is spreading infection or pyrexia.

## Why

Metronidazole is highly active against the strict anaerobes (Bacteroides, Fusobacterium, Prevotella) that drive odontogenic infection alongside streptococci. It works by being reduced inside anaerobic cells to a DNA-damaging radical — aerobic cells cannot activate it, which is why it spares most commensals and is safe in penicillin allergy. When added to amoxicillin in a severe spreading infection, the two drugs give complementary cover for both the streptococcal and anaerobic populations.

## How this applies in practice

- Adult dose: 400 mg TDS for 5 days (15 x 400 mg tablets).
- Warn the patient: no alcohol during the course or for 48 hours after — metronidazole produces a disulfiram-like reaction (flushing, nausea, palpitations).
- Do NOT prescribe to anyone on warfarin — metronidazole inhibits warfarin metabolism and the INR rises sharply, with bleeding risk.
- Not licensed under 1 year.
- For NUG and pericoronitis the regimen is shorter: metronidazole 400 mg TDS for 3 days alongside scaling/irrigation.
