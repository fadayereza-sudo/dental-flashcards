## The recommendation

For under-18s use a simplified BPE on six index teeth — UR6, UR1, UL6, LL6, LL1 and LR6 — with a WHO 621 probe. In 7–11 year olds (mixed dentition) use only codes 0–2; from 12–17 years (permanent dentition) the full code set 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 and * applies.

## Why

False pockets around erupting teeth in the mixed dentition would generate spurious codes 3 and 4, so the protocol restricts younger children to the codes that flag plaque, calculus and inflammation rather than depth. Limiting screening to six index teeth keeps the appointment tolerable for children while still sampling the high-risk sites that mirror adult patterns. The second black band on the WHO 621 probe (8.5–11.5 mm) is useful in this group because eruption-related false pockets can be deep without representing true attachment loss.

## How this applies in practice

- Probe only UR6, UR1, UL6, LL6, LL1 and LR6 — not the full dentition.
- Under 12: codes 0, 1 or 2 only. If the pocket appears deeper, recheck after eruption is complete rather than recording 3 or 4.
- 12 and over: full adult code set including 3, 4 and *.
- Aggressive periodontitis, incipient chronic perio not responding to treatment, drug-induced gingival overgrowth, or any genetic/systemic condition predisposing to perio destruction in a child = referral to specialist services.
