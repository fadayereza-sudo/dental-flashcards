## The recommendation

For Duraphat 22,600 ppm varnish, use 0.25 ml for children approximately 2 to 5 years old (nursery and Primary 1) and 0.4 ml for children approximately 5 years and over (Primary 2 and above).

## Why

Fluoride varnish is highly concentrated, so the correct volume per age is the safety margin against ingestion of a toxic dose. The split at age 5 matches both dentition (still mostly primary below 5, first permanent molars erupting and larger surface area from 5 upwards) and body weight. Using the adult 0.4 ml dose in a 3-year-old can push the fluoride load above safe limits if the child swallows residual varnish; using 0.25 ml on a 10-year-old under-treats the larger dentition.

## How this applies in practice

- Write the volume on the tray alongside the varnish — 0.25 ml for ages 2–5, 0.4 ml for 5+.
- The same volume cap applies when combining SDF with a varnish finish — don't exceed the age-appropriate Duraphat dose.
- Prioritise proximal surfaces in primary teeth — those surfaces are particularly prone to caries and are easy to miss.
- Dry each quadrant thoroughly before applying — dry enamel lets the varnish set into the surface.
- Advise the family that soft food and drink are fine from 30 minutes after application, but no tooth brushing or hard food for 4 hours so the varnish stays put.
