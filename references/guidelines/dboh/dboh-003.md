## The recommendation

Apply sodium fluoride 22,600 ppm (2.26% NaF) varnish twice a year to all children attending the dental surgery, regardless of water fluoridation status.

## Why

Twice-yearly varnish produces an average 37% reduction in caries increment in the primary dentition and 43% in the permanent dentition (multiple systematic reviews). This is the largest evidence-based gain available from a single in-surgery intervention for children, and it works on top of (not instead of) twice-daily fluoride toothpaste. Most of the trial evidence used 22,600 ppm specifically — that's why the concentration is named in the recommendation. The benefit holds even in fluoridated areas, because varnish delivers a high topical dose to the tooth surface that water fluoridation cannot match.

## How this applies in practice

- Apply at routine checks in line with the recall interval (usually 6-monthly for children).
- A trained dental nurse can apply the varnish on a dentist's prescription — use the team's skill mix to make this routine, not exceptional.
- Plaque doesn't need a full prophy beforehand; remove gross plaque only.
- Increasingly worth offering to higher-risk adults with retained natural teeth — frail older patients especially. The aesthetic objection that limits fluoride for adults doesn't apply, since varnish washes off.
- If you skip varnish for a child without a documented contraindication, you are leaving the highest-evidence preventive intervention on the table.
