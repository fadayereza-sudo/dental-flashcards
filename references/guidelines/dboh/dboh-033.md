## The recommendation

After active periodontal therapy, deliver supportive periodontal care (SPC) consisting of plaque/gingival monitoring, probing depth and bleeding-on-probing assessment, oral-hygiene reinforcement, professional mechanical plaque removal, and root surface debridement of pockets ≥5 mm with bleeding on probing.

## Why

Periodontitis is a chronic disease that recurs and worsens without sustained plaque control. Trials show no clear winner among SPC protocols, but no trial has compared SPC to no SPC because withholding maintenance after active treatment is unethical given the recurrence risk. The components are designed to detect deterioration early (so any new active disease is caught and re-treated), reinforce the patient's home care (because professional removal of supra and subgingival deposits is of limited value without high standards of plaque control), and target professional debridement only where it is needed (pockets ≥5 mm with bleeding, not every tooth every visit).

## How this applies in practice

- Set the patient's expectations up front: SPC is a long-term commitment, not a one-off course of treatment.
- At each SPC visit, run through: plaque score, gingival inflammation, probing depths and bleeding on probing, full pocket chart annually.
- Reinforce oral hygiene and behaviour change at every visit — this is the load-bearing part.
- PMPR removes supra and subgingival plaque and calculus; root surface debridement targets pockets ≥5 mm with BoP.
- Recall interval set by individual risk and stability — typically 3 months for unstable patients, longer for stable ones.
- A patient who fails to engage with home care will not maintain stability however thorough the professional debridement.
