## What it is

The Simplified BPE (sBPE) is the periodontal screening method for under-18s. To avoid the false-pocket problem from erupting permanent teeth, it assesses **six index teeth**: all four first permanent molars plus UR1 and LL1.

- **Probe:** WHO probe with 0.5 mm ball end and black band at 3.5 to 5.5 mm
- **Method:** Assess 6 points per tooth (db, b, mb, dl, l, ml)

The 2017 World Workshop classification eliminated "chronic" and "aggressive" periodontitis as diagnoses; the 2021 sBPE guidance is built around the 2017/2018 categories (Stage I–IV, Grade A–C, Periodontitis: Molar–Incisor Pattern).

## Index teeth (Figure 1a)

- **Upper:** UR6, UR1, UL6
- **Lower:** LR6, LL1, LL6

## sBPE codes (Figure 1b)

| Code | Definition |
|---|---|
| 0 | Healthy |
| 1 | Bleeding after gentle probing. Black band fully visible |
| 2 | Calculus or plaque retention factor. Black band fully visible |
| 3 | Shallow pocket 4 mm or 5 mm. Black band partly visible |
| 4 | Deep pocket 6 mm or more. Black band disappears |
| * | Furcation involvement |

### Age-banded code use

- **7–11 years (mixed dentition):** Use sBPE codes **0, 1, 2** on the index teeth only
- **12–17 years (permanent dentition):** Use sBPE codes **0, 1, 2, 3, 4 and \*** on the index teeth

---

## Table 1 — Summary guidance on interpretation

| sBPE Code | Action |
|---|---|
| **0** | No periodontal treatment. Screen again at routine recall or within **1 year**, whichever sooner. |
| **1** | Oral hygiene instruction (OHI). Screen again at routine recall or within **6 months**, whichever sooner. |
| **2** | OHI as for Code 1. Supragingival/subgingival professional mechanical plaque removal (PMPR). Remove/manage plaque retention factors. Screen again at routine recall or within **6 months**, whichever sooner. |
| **3** | OHI as for Codes 1 and 2. Supragingival/subgingival PMPR with particular emphasis on **subgingival PMPR in shallow 4–5 mm pockets**. Remove/manage plaque retention factors. **After 3 months**, do a full periodontal assessment including **6-point PPD chart in affected sextants**. |
| **4 or \*** | **Unusual in young patients.** Do a full periodontal assessment including 6-point PPD chart throughout the **entire dentition**. **Consider referral to a Specialist**, while doing initial therapy, as Code 3. |

---

## Table 2 — When to refer to Specialist Services (under-18s)

GDP: Consider specialist referral in younger ages when any of the following apply:

1. **Stage II, III periodontitis not responding to treatment**
2. **Grade C or Stage IV periodontitis**
3. Medical history that significantly affects periodontal treatment or requiring multi-disciplinary care
4. **Periodontitis as a direct manifestation of systemic disease**
5. Systemic/genetic diseases that can affect periodontal supporting tissues
6. Root morphology/furcation defects adversely affecting prognosis on key teeth
7. Non-plaque-induced conditions requiring complex or specialist care
8. Cases requiring diagnosis/management of rare/complex clinical pathology
9. **Drug-induced gingival overgrowth needing surgery**
10. Cases requiring evaluation for periodontal surgery
